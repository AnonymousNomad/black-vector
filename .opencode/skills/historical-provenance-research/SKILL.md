# HISTORICAL PROVENANCE RESEARCH (BV-SKILL-023)

## NAME
HISTORICAL PROVENANCE RESEARCH

## PURPOSE
Make BLACK VECTOR's use of real history verifiable and lawful. This skill owns the research standard for any
historically-grounded claim: primary-source-first hierarchy, mandatory evidence classification, the
archive/records workflow, the institutional-program patterns (successor folding, records destruction, the
"name changes, work continues" reflex), and the provenance chains that convert real history into fiction at
named, labeled seams (directive D011-R; SOURCE_STANDARD.md). It is the gate a directive must pass BEFORE any
unsourced historical claim, program reference, or timeline beat reaches canon. Complementary,
non-overlapping owners: anomalous-consciousness-research (BV-SKILL-026) owns the paranormal/consciousness
investigation boundary; this skill owns everything else and the methodology itself.

## WHEN TO LOAD
- Writing or editing any historically grounded claim: programs, agencies, operations, transfers, experiments,
  timelines, or institutional backstories (Black Hand lineage, Compound, Shade lineages).
- Creating the D012 Black Hand historical/provenance timeline or any historical narrative beats.
- Editing the provenance chains (SOURCE_STANDARD.md §5) or any ledger source-record.
- Any directive that would otherwise freehand a "real fact."
- Reconciling archive findings against established doctrine (SOURCES vs assumptions).

## DO NOT LOAD WHEN
- Anomalous-intelligence / consciousness-program investigation work → BV-SKILL-026 owns that boundary.
- Gameplay/dialogue/mechanics authoring with no historical grounding claims (e.g., pure QoL improvements).
- Visual-equipment work → BV-SKILL-022.

## PRECONDITIONS
- Governing set loaded; SOP-002 (Scope & Authority) routing confirms research is in directive scope.
- `docs/research/SOURCE_STANDARD.md` present and consulted.
- For explicit canvass work (specific program/site/event): authorizes whether live web/lookup research is
  permitted in the directive's scope (D011-R does; later directives default to classify-first without new lookup).
- Asimov-style freshness: no live-source reliance without a re-verification pass (see VERIFICATION).

## GOVERNING INVARIANTS
1. PRIMARY-FIRST HIERARCHY (§SOURCE_STANDARD 1): NARA/CIA CREST/DoD/DOE/VA/NPS/LoC/universities before academic
   secondary before popular. Popular = discovery only, never canon basis.
2. EVERY SIGNIFICANT CLAIM IS CLASSIFIED (§2): DOCUMENTED (primary-confirmed) / DISPUTED INCONCLUSIVE
   (conflicting-or-single-sourced/self-assessed) / FICTIONALIZED DERIVATION (real node + fiction materialization,
   seam shown) / PURE BLACK VECTOR FICTION (invented, labeled, no real sources cited). No claim escapes a label.
3. EXISTENCE ≠ EFFICACY: documenting that a program existed never inherits its claims. Program success quoted is
   always rendered as claimed-by / internal-assessment.
4. INSTITUTION ABSORBS, IT DOES NOT BELONG: no "Nazis survived" secret-base fantasy (doctrine §19) — transfer
   of wartime personnel/knowledge/technology is how institutions work; occult-mythology layers must be presented
   AS mythology with a visible seam. BLACK VECTOR is the path the Cold War never got to walk, never a copy of it.
5. NO ACTIONABLE CONTENT: no synthesis, no dose/effect mapping (Compound), no construction/mod/manual, no
   torture/interrogation/abuse how-to, no attack instructions, no large copyrighted paste. Summary + source names.
6. NO REAL THING REPRODUCED (doctrine §16/§17): no real facility/unit/person lifted; Alaska sites inform Simse
   Sound as reference templates only; invention always labeled.
7. THE SEAM IS THE STORY: transitions from real history to fiction are method-opaque in the document and story,
   but the ledger must name the exact node. Provenance chains (SOURCE_STANDARD §5) terminate at a named
   divergence: one lineage obtains repeatable anomalous results real history never established (doctrine §22-25).

## WORKFLOW
1. Scope the claim: IDENTIFY the fact → DETERMINE provenance (primary near-primary, secondary, or none) → if
   none in scope and no lookup authorized, classify as PURE FICTION or strip the claim (Stop option).
2. Query the hierarchy in order (when lookup authorized): NARA → CIA CREST → DoD/DOE/VA → NPS/state → LoC →
   universities → reputable secondary. Chase primary identifiers (record series, report numbers, FTs).
3. Cross-check multiparty: if only self-assessment or a single source, upgrade to DISPUTED INCONCLUSIVE.
4. Classify EVERY claim; write the source-record (SOURCE_STANDARD §4 format) into the relevant ledger file:
   HISTORICAL_PROGRAMS, ALASKA_SITES, MILITARY_ROLES, WEAPON_REFERENCE_FAMILIES, HUMAN_MACHINE_SYSTEMS,
   CONSCIOUSNESS_ANOMALOUS (via 026), or WILDLIFE_ECOLOGY.
5. Route fiction authoring: cite the ledger item; confirm the seam label + provenance node before any narrative beat.
6. Close the loop: update registry cross-links; re-run methodology validation; surface contradictions to doctrine.

## IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE
- Concretize "the successor machine": a program folding into its successor while keeping its work (BLUEBIRD →
  ARTICHOKE; then the MK-tradition; the anomalous cluster → GRILL FLAME→…→STAR GATE) is the institution's
  signature move — reuse it for Hand compartment shopping, never present it as a scandal beyond what is real.
- Records-destruction (Helms 1973 MKULTRA memo) is a documented, non-mystical habit — reuse it as reflexive
  erasure in the fiction (DOCUMENTED as action, not as "they hid the truth," which is fiction's job to label).
- Nazi-era medical/experiment material: present as human-destruction history; the Ahnenerbe-esque occult layer
  stays labeled mythology (a fusion is allowed where the seam is visible: an institution would take the money,
  the expertise, and the safety-filtered doctrine, not the mysticism).
- The DIVERGENCE (anomalous results real history never established) is the single fiction funnel; everything
  after it is pure fiction authored under this skill's label rules.
- Keep version discipline: a ledger edit re-runs validate_methodology; any change to SOURCE_STANDARD is a
  methodology change and requires full routing.

## ANTI-PATTERNS
- Presenting MKULTRA/Edgewood/STAR GATE-era programs as proven in their claimed effects (they are DISPUTED).
- Rendering the Ahnenerbe mystical layer as evidence (it is classified mythology; labeled).
- Using popular articles as canon basis; copying substantial excerpts from copyrighted sources.
- Claiming a civilian "secret Nazi base" / "Nazis survived" in ANY un-fictionalized framing (doctrine §19).
- Writing the Compound's chemistry/sourcing/dosing anywhere on-paper (PURE FICTION; no actionable detail).
- Lifting a real facility name, unit designator, or individual into the fiction.
- Ending a provenance chain without a labeled divergence node.

## KNOWN FAILURE MODES
- Drift from DISPUTED back to DOCUMENTED on a good-sounding source — re-check primary identifiers.
- Seam blur: FICTIONALIZED DERIVATION written so smoothly the reader cannot tell real from invented — the
  ledger label must always survive, even when story text is subtle.
- "Apparently documented" pass-down where the original is actually a journalist's claim — verify to record.
- Ledger sprawl: keep per-item records dense and finite; when a family needs its own file, create via routing.

## VERIFICATION
- Static: every source-record has item/category/classification/sources/BV use/must-not-claim/doctrine-skill links;
  no uncategorized historical claim in canon; ledger edits pass `tools/validate_methodology.py`.
- Sampling: for any 3 claims, trace to a named primary/near-primary identifier; confirm existence-vs-efficacy split.
- Contradiction watch: any historical claim that would contradict an established doctrine assumption is surfaced
  to the routing agent before proceeding (Stop option).

## STOP CONDITIONS
If a directive asks for an unsourced historical claim, to present program efficacy as fact, to author the
Compound's chemistry/dosing, to reproduce a real facility/person, or to write an unlabeled occult-mysticism
layer — stop and re-anchor to SOURCE_STANDARD §3/§5 before continuing. Do not continue the directive around the
stop; route to the authority that can clarify scope.
