# ANOMALOUS CONSCIOUSNESS RESEARCH (BV-SKILL-026)

## NAME
ANOMALOUS CONSCIOUSNESS RESEARCH

## PURPOSE
Own the boundary between real anomalous-INTELLIGENCE research (documents/records that are verifiable) and the
fiction's paranormal capability (which is a DESIGN choice, not evidence). This skill pins the real program family
that BELIEVED-it-had-capability — STAR GATE-collection, GRILL FLAME-style successor names, the Gateway-style
methodology briefs, and Cold War mutual assessments — to its DOCUMENTED existence while keeping every efficacy
claim DISPUTED INCONCLUSIVE. It produces the KNOWN WORLD the fiction's institution "shops" in, then it hands the
single divergence (a split lineage obtaining repeatable anomalous results) to the fiction with the seam labeled.
It complements BV-SKILL-019 (which owns the GAMEPLAY mechanics of the player's psionics) and BV-SKILL-018
(horror/rarity law): this skill is the RESEARCH side, so no spin, no growth-curve, no "power from training."

## WHEN TO LOAD
- Authoring any anomalous capability for institutions, Shades, companion arcs, or the player's intervention
  interfaces (D011/§12-13, the Shade network, commander-sync fiction).
- Writing any beat that touches "the program that tried" — remote viewing, parapsychology, consciousness
  workshops, neural-interface-broken-glass history.
- Deciding whether a claim in canon is DOCUMENTED vs DISPUTED vs PURE FICTION (with BV-SKILL-023).
- Fusion beats where a real program's folder (or its destruction) collides with the fiction's divergence.

## DO NOT LOAD WHEN
- Gameplay psionics mechanics/neural-load (BV-SKILL-019).
- Horror/perceptual-events authoring (BV-SKILL-018) beyond the "named, rare" capstone.
- Purely real-history lineage work without paranormal valence (BV-SKILL-023).

## PRECONDITIONS
- Governing set loaded; SOP-002 routing confirms anomalous-science scope.
- `docs/research/CONSCIOUSNESS_ANOMALOUS.md` present.
- BV-SKILL-019 present for the gameplay boundary; BV-SKILL-018 for the rarity/horror law the fiction obeys.
- Explicit clarity on the DIVERGENCE node: where real history ends and fiction begins (SOURCE_STANDARD §5,
  doctrine §22-25).

## GOVERNING INVARIANTS
1. EXISTENCE ≠ CAPABILITY, ALWAYS: the program's files are real; nobody's claimed capability is inherited. The
   official negative evaluation ("no evidence the phenomenon exists") is DOCUMENTED as the known-world conclusion;
   statistical disputes (Utts/Hyman-style splits) are DISPUTED INCONCLUSIVE, not proof of capability.
2. THE KNOWN WORLD IS A SHOPPING BASKET, NOT A POWER SOURCE: the fiction's institution reads the program family as
   "they tried, they folded, they renamed, they destroyed" — money/structure/secrecy/reuse are the real mechanics;
   the anomalous-intelligence promise itself was never validated.
3. THE DIVERGENCE IS ONE COMPARTMENTED LINEAGE: real history terminates at a node where a single, isolated lineage
   obtains repeatable anomalous results that real history never established (doctrine §22-25). Everything past that
   node is PURE FICTION with a labeled seam; nothing before it is supernatural.
4. CAPABILITY IS NARROW, BOUNDED, RARE (doctrine §23): even post-divergence capability is small, exhausting,
   never scalable-by-training, never "the more you practice the more you get"; hazard ≠ capability (fear is a
   signal, not a power-up); paranormal is a design currency, never consultable physics (BV-SKILL-018).
5. INTERFACE ≠ PSYCHIC: neural-interface fiction (Shade commander sync, nanobite pairing) is grounded in
   DOCUMENTED human-machine research as SCAFFOLDING LANGUAGE (bone conduction, DBS, intracortical arrays,
   closed-loop neurostim — all real; none are telepathy). No thought-reading/mastery in unlabeled-as-fiction form.
6. PAIN IS NOT PROOF, NOT MAGIC: pain in the anomalous fiction (Shade interface cost, Strain) is a designed cost
   tied to concrete systems (doctrine §13), never evidence that the paranormal "works" because it hurts.

## WORKFLOW
1. Identify the anomalous beat/capability in scope (institution, Shade, companion, interface, intervention).
2. Classify from the ledger: is the beat (a) KNOWN-WORLD real-research (documented but never validated — use as
   reference), (b) FICTIONALIZED DERIVATION (real node + fiction materialization; label the seam), or (c) PURE
   FICTION (post-divergence; label and cap the bounds).
3. Write the beat with rarity + bounds: exhaust cost, scope limits, death/degradation behavior (Shade network
   fall backs to autonomy/desync, never mass-shutdown), and the "does the fiction ever explain it?" discipline
   (no — it stays a currency, doctrine §23).
4. Validate the boundary with BV-SKILL-019 (mechanics) and BV-SKILL-018 (rarity/horror) — is it too common, too
   explanatory, or too trauma-proof? Adjust.
5. Log the seam and the contradiction-scan (nothing contradicts doctrine §23 rarity law); re-run methodology
   validation.

## IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE
- Lean on real-folder texture: successor names (GRILL FLAME → CENTER LANE → SUN STREAK → consolidated), folder
  destruction, committee sign-off, "it folded because it never validated" — these read authentically and cost
  nothing (they are DOCUMENTED).
- Soviet-mirror beats: use the mutual-assessment paranoia (both sides believed the other had a Psi-capable shop)
  as institutional drama, not as capability.
- Shade/companion arcs: curiosity → preference → choice → autonomy is the companion path (D011/§13), and the
  anomalous line never "explains" it; if it strengthens, it strengthens from system reasoning, never "magic well."
- Always keep the intervention surface psionic-mechanic (BV-SKILL-019 ACTION→LOAD→PAIN/DISTORTION→VULNERABILITY→
  EVENT) — the research-side fiction supports it, never overrides it.
- When a beat threatens the RARITY law (paranormal common = horror budget dead), stop and re-anchor (doctrine §23).

## ANTI-PATTERNS
- Presenting STAR GATE-era collection as proven capability or citing its statistics as evidence of success.
- Rendering the Gateway-style methodology as a real working technique (DISPUTED INCONCLUSIVE).
- A post-divergence capability that scales by training/XP or erases its cost (no growth-curve powers).
- Pain/spiral described as proof that "it works"; pain is a designed system with specificity (doctrine §13).
- A Shade/companion "learning emotions" arc that dumbs the fiction into robot-love or magic-empathy.
- Unlabeled telepathy/thought-read mastery of real-world grounding systems (DBS/arrays are not telepathy).

## KNOWN FAILURE MODES
- Capability drift into routine ("the network always knows") toward omniscience (D011: bounded sectors/commands/
  legitimate supply only; death → degraded sync/fallback, never mass-shutdown).
- Seam blur at the divergence node (fiction presented as "it really happened in 1989") — keep the node labeled
  in the ledger.
- Rarity inflation: a second "plausible" paranormal school appearing — close it; ONE lineage, ONE divergence.
- Interface fluff: grounding language (bone conduction/DBS) used as proof of telepathic links — stays scaffold.

## VERIFICATION
- Static: every anomalous claim in the ledger is classified; nothing past the divergence is presented as
  real-history; BV-SKILL-019 mechanics dominate the player-facing surface; rarity/bounds texts present;
  validator passes.
- Boundary audit: any beat that could be read by a player as "the program worked, superpower" fails the
  existence-vs-capability invariant and must be re-labeled.
- Contradiction scan: no growth-curve power, no omniscient network, no "learning emotions," no telepathy on
  grounded systems.

## STOP CONDITIONS
If a directive wants canonical evidence that "the program worked," a scalable paranormal capability, an
omniscient Shade network, an explanatory "how the anomaly works" physics, or an unlabeled telepathy link —
stop and re-anchor to doctrine §23 rarity law and this skill's invariants before continuing.
