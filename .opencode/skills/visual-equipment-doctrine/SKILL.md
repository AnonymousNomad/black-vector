# VISUAL & EQUIPMENT DOCTRINE (BV-SKILL-022)

## NAME
VISUAL & EQUIPMENT DOCTRINE

## PURPOSE
Own the visual/equipment language frozen in doctrine §41 (Directive 010). This skill is the selectable authority for every human-military character/armor/equipment visual decision: the global armor language (military-authentic first, advanced technology second, anomalous last), The Hand's Hidden Hand military silhouette, the Shade baseline and its tiers, role-based armor families, the digital-camo/soft-suit family, cybernetic augmentation limits, faction separation, Alaska serviceability, the wear/damage spectrum, the technology escalation ladder, color/material discipline, and the originality boundary that treats reference material as language only (BV-D042).

## WHEN TO LOAD
- Authoring or reviewing any armor, uniform, headgear, load-bearing, or character visual for enemies, faction units, The Hand, or Shade (including the companion Shade).
- Designing a faction or enemy family's on-screen identity, silhouettes, or material signatures.
- Translating any real-world or external reference into an original BLACK VECTOR design.
- Any decision touching the technology ladder (T0–T4), cybernetic visibility zones, or later nanobite-linked visuals.

## DO NOT LOAD WHEN
- Gameplay implementation without a visual/equipment question (CQC, AI, survival, progression — load their owning skills).
- World/environment architecture and building visuals (BV-SKILL-006, large-world-sector-architecture; the Alaska world foundation doc).
- Rendering/performance choices for presented materials (mobile-graphics-atmosphere + SOP-004).
- Character memory/progression or enemy AI behavior itself.

## PRECONDITIONS
- Governing set loaded.
- Doctrinal authority read: doctrine §41 (Directive 010 visual freeze), §4/§9 (starting lock / weapon doctrine), §14-§20 (characters and factions), §29 (prologue), §30-§31 (wilderness and facilities), §33 (reference responsibility map), §35.7 (AI-facing roles list), SOP-006.
- Reference stack accepted as design language only (BV-D042); no copyable asset is in scope.

## GOVERNING INVARIANTS
1. NORTH STAR (BV-D042/D043): military-authentic first, advanced technology second, anomalous/paranormal last; armor must read as recognizable tactical equipment that EVOLVED, with technology integrated into it, not pasted on; restrained functional illumination only.
2. SHADE BASELINE (BV-D044): visibly human — exposed face out of combat, implant/conditioning damage, scarred neural-interface areas, flattened expression; retractable segmented combat helmet (collar/upper-back/temple storage); rifle defines him more than the blade; compact powered/stun/vibro blade is secondary CQC only; no katana/samurai dominance.
3. SHADE TIERS (BV-D045): LOW/SPECIALIST/COMMAND/COMPANION differ by PURPOSE, never by weight; the companion derives from the lower-tier system, accumulates repair/individualization, and stays network-readable.
4. ROLE LEGIBILITY (BV-D046): every role family (stealth/recon, assault, containment, sniper/overwatch, technical/electronic, commander, heavy support, cold-weather patrol, facility security, recovery/extraction) carries coherent silhouette/armor-distribution/headgear/clothing-layer/sensor/density/mobility/weather logic; no palette-swapped clones.
5. CYBERNETIC CEILING (BV-D047): augmentation confined to neck, skull interface, forearms, knees, lower legs, spine-support areas, sensor mounts; human anatomy readable; whole-body machinery only for narrative-justified subjects.
6. FRACTION SEPARATION (BV-D048): Hidden Hand / Black Hand security / Shades / mercenary-contractor / broken subjects are distinguished by silhouette, layering, and material signature — never identifiable by color alone.
7. SERVICEABLE IN ALASKA (BV-D043): every family accounts for cold, wetness, salt, snow, wind, darkness, maritime conditions, mud, industrial interiors, remote maintenance; no showroom armor except narratively intentional.
8. WEAR SPECTRUM (BV-D049): PRISTINE → SERVICEABLE → DAMAGED → CRITICAL → FAILED; equipment visibly scratches, stains, tears, delaminates, loses attachments, exposes underlayers; Shade gear accumulates history; the companion Shade evolves with modification/repair.
9. TECHNOLOGY LADDER (BV-D050): T0 conventional → T1 enhanced → T2 networked → T3 experimental → T4 anomalous-interface; T4 is never everyday equipment.
10. ORIGINALITY (BV-D042): external/uploaded visuals are reference language only — extract layering, silhouette, equipment density, cybernetic integration, material balance, camouflage treatment, helmet concepts; design original variants; never reproduce a specific design.

## WORKFLOW
1. Determine faction + role + tier for the design; read the matching doctrine §41 sub-section and this skill's invariants.
2. Define the silhouette first (readability at range without color), then armor distribution, headgear, layers, sensors, equipment density, mobility, and weather adaptation.
3. Place the design on the technology ladder (T0–T4); keep the cybernetic zones within BV-D047 and the restrained-illumination rule.
4. Apply the Alaska serviceability rule and a target wear state from the PRISTINE→FAILED spectrum; if the subject is a Shade, record how the design derives from and stays readable against the network.
5. Verify against the original regenerated variant test: the design must be original BLACK VECTOR material, not a reference reproduction, and must satisfy the north-star test.

## IMPLEMENTATION GUIDANCE
- Read people before gear: a silhouette's readability comes from the head/neck, shoulders, and gear bulk profile first.
- Distinguish by material/signature rather than color: oxide-rich Black Hand steel vs worn olive Hidden Hand kit vs texture-rich mercenary commercial mix.
- Keep illuminated accents communicative: a helmet HUD glint or an interface seam is state; a glowing trim line on an unpowered area is decoration.
- For the companion Shade, layer history: repaired plate, replaced pouch, swap marks, weathering — over an intact low-tier architecture, so origin stays legible (BV-D045/D049).
- Enemy gear is never player gear: the Shade rifle canon does not relax the player starting lock (doctrine §4/§9, §41.4 boundary).
- Use the desaturated original pixel-noise pattern family for soft-suit/stealth roles; never adopt a real-world protected camouflage pattern (BV-D042/D051).

## ANTI-PATTERNS
- Cyberpunk-costume soldiers, superhero armor plates that read as a costume, full mecha silhouettes, interchangeable "future operator" defaults (BV-D043).
- Tech pasted on unconvincingly or decorative neon trim that communicates nothing (BV-D043).
- Shade drawn as a robot/android/faceless cyborg, or a katana-dominant silhouette (BV-D044).
- Shade tiers expressed as "heavier armor up the ladder" instead of purpose-driven difference (BV-D045).
- Role families that are identical meshes with swapped colors (BV-D046).
- Full-body machinery on a default human, or augmentation that hides anatomy (BV-D047).
- Any faction only identifiable by bright color (BV-D048/D051).
- Showroom-clean gear in Alaska without narrative intent (BV-D043).
- Copying a specific armor/helmet/character/commercial design from the reference stack (BV-D042).

## KNOWN FAILURE MODES
- Drift toward "heavier = better tier" for Shade — re-anchor to purpose (BV-D045).
- Silhouette hampering readability: identical head+shoulder profile across roles — enforce headgear/neck language first (BV-D046).
- Over-lighting accent elements on the GL Compatibility budget — function-only glow, cheap emissive discipline (SOP-004).
- Reference creep: a studied image slipping into a composite that reproduces the original — keep the extraction-to-variant step explicit (BV-D042).
- Design a faction by palette instead of structural/material signature, breaking the dark/muted tone (BV-D051).

## VERIFICATION
- Static (doctrine/skill level): invariants present; each design artifact can answer faction, role, tier, tech-tier, wear-state, serviceability, and originality-north-star questions; prohibited visual anti-patterns absent from written specs.
- Behavioral (when asset production is authorized later): orthographic/mesh passes are readable at silhouette scale by an unfamiliar viewer; two role families confuse no one; a shade renders as human in the emergent low-tier default.

## STOP CONDITIONS
If a proposed visual reads as generic cyberpunk/superhero/mecha, reproduces a reference design, turns Shade into a faceless machine, or identifies a faction by color — stop and re-anchor to doctrine §41 before any asset work.
