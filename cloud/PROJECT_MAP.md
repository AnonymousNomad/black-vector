## Product law

The dense main island remains the primary playable world.

Real Alaska geography is structural inspiration and broader operational context.

Do not stretch the island into a low-density whole-Alaska open world merely to connect future locations.

Gameplay state outranks animation state.

Transition != projection for persisted state.

World truth != NPC perception != NPC belief != identity confidence.

Concept art != gameplay authorization.

Capability != acceptance.

Build success != player acceptance.
