> Purpose: role abstraction that D011/§3-8 population taxonomy and specific operators consume. Roles are
> DESIGN abstractions (function, load, tempo, tell), not "real table-forces." Feeds BV-SKILL-023/025, doctrine

## Role families (abstraction for behavior/load/rhythm design)

1. QUARTERBACK (embedded command): network/tactical control; short-medium engagement; high cognitive load.
2. POINT / BREACHER: entry schemes, low-shot-high-risk; loud, holographic, visual-heavy (fits BV-SKILL-022 cones).
3. MARKSMAN / OVERWATCH: medium-long rifle work + surveillance; low rate, high patience; the overwatch seam.
4. GUNNER: sustained area denial, recoil/manage-oriented; function of platform more than skill.
5. COUNTER-SNIPER / SOFT TARGET: the "hunter vs hunter" niche; patience + reading terrain, not speed.
6. SUPPORT / LOGISTICS: resupply, casualty, tech; low combat density but needed — the "nothing advances without
   the band-aid box" tension.
7. RECON / SCOUT: stealth, route knowledge, terrain-reading; pairs with wildlife-trail knowledge storytelling.
8. QRF/HEAVY (rapid-response): speed-response team; cold-professional tempo; ties to D011/§3-5 garrison doctrine.

## The Hand's sniper/overwatch capability (§8)

SOURCE RECORD
- Item: Elite overwatch/sniper capability, extended-shift vigilance, high-perception fieldcraft
- Classification: FICTIONALIZED DERIVATION (design abstraction; created from documented elite-marksman/overwatch
  professional practice). See guard below.
- Sources: professional/service sniper/observer training references (verbal description, no field manual); military
  history secondary (patience/terrain-reading discipline, e.g., service-level marksmanship history books)
- BV use: The Hand pair's signature capability sits at the EDGE of superior training: extraordinary patience,
  observation, load discipline, calm under punishment — never superhuman marksmanship or supernatural sensing
- Must not claim (CRITICAL): that this capability is or was retroactively supernatural (doctrine §23-25 —
  the Hand's anomalous divergence is elsewhere and rare). No augmented-vision system, no "psychic aim" — the
  advantage is skill + perception applied to real geometry
- Relevant: doctrine §8/§23, D011/§8, BV-SKILL-025

## Role-to-fiction mapping rules
- Roles populate factions (D011 enemy families) by FUNCTION not by "class list": a given operator is
  function + faction + equipment + behavior + condition + modifier (D011/§13 compositional rule).
- Overwatch/mediator archetypes at compound boundaries lean on role 3; inside-warriors in the hole lean on
  roles 1/2/4; garrison/lab staff on roles 6/7; QRF on 8.
- Boss contracts (D011): every boss = a role saturated across tells/rules/limits/counterplay — never a
  stat-skin.
- Ledger-only: designation language everywhere in the fiction is BOXED terminology (QVRS-8, Delta-1 style):
  a fictional naming language that never collides with real NATO/JSOC designations.
