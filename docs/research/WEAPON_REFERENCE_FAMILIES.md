## Platform families (role-lead, not name-lead)

1. CQB CARBINE — short-burst interior work; matches Point/Breacher role.
2. MODULAR GPC CARBINE — role/optic/length-swap core; "many tanks one platform" storytelling;
   ✓ kept as desired future (a good platform for layered upgrades).
3. RECON / LOW-SIGNATURE — short action, suppressed line; pairs with Recon role; the Hand's quiet overwatch kit.
4. BATTLE RIFLE — full-power tradition, recoil-character, older stock in good hands (institution legacy feel).
5. DMR — long-range designated marksman polyline; middle of battle rifle vs precision rifle.
6. PRECISION RIFLE — The Hand's overwatch/marksman signature; build, patience, geometry (role 3 above).
7. HEAVY PRECISION — barrier/severe-caliber behavior for boss contracts.
8. PDW — compact machine-pistol degree of compactness for close/vehicle/campaign.
9. COMBAT SHOTGUN — close-fight patterns and breaching flavor.
10. CIVILIAN HUNTING RIFLE — D011/§6 civilian hunting-class weapons (foursquare legacy, older lines, upkeep
    visible), sharing family DNA but not military standardized.
11. SPECULATIVE NETWORKED RIFLE — integrated optics/sensor/display rail, longer-action "commander's rifle" —
    EXPLICITLY FICTION (no real version exists at that integration level). The Shade-era end of the fiction's
    gun line. Uses the existing psionic/neural interface fiction (BV-SKILL-019/026), still no supernatural ballistics.

## Provenance ecology (why a platform exists in-fiction)
- Each family carries a STORY of assignment lineage: research-fleet inherited gear vs Hand-compartment gear vs
  civilian/seized gear — do not freehand wave-1/unlocking progression. Faction identity: Hand platforms read
  "institution-maintained, same-family, cosmetic variance" vs garrison mixed inventory vs civilian legacy single
  long-guns (D011/§6).
- Wear/logistics: an isolated platform (D010, BV-D043) runs on parts + care culture, not resupply lines —
  favor RELIABLE-TO-FIX and REUSABLE platforms, low exotic parts demand; this is a design constraint for D011/§6.

## Representation/underrepresented traditions (originality d)
- Deliberately include underrepresented-but-real traditions in family skins and ergonomics: bullpups, roller-delayed
  actions, Czech/European leaf, scout-rifle hybrid concept (weight/walkability), pre-1960 pyrophoric lines.
- Intent: avoid the "AR-15 in every hand" monotone in the fiction's equipment art (BV-SKILL-022) without violating
  realism rules. These are ART/HISTORY flavors, not gameplay stat swings.
