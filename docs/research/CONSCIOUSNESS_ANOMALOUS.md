## Fiction boundary (critical)

- All of the above forms the KNOWN WORLD (what the institution across the Cold War "knew"/spent on).
- THE DIVERGENCE (doctrine §22-25, SOURCE_STANDARD §5): a single compartmented lineage obtains
  repeatable, testable anomalous results that real history never established. That is the moment the
  fiction owns the story. Nothing earlier is supernatural.
- Rarity law (doctrine §23): even the post-divergence capability is narrow, bounded, exhausting, and
  never "the more you train the more power." Hazard ≠ capability. Hand anomaly operators are the
  exception, not the result of the program.
- The paranormal is a design currency, never a physics the characters can consult.
