  HUD/helmet-mount display programs, wide industrial AR). Fiction mounts "system feedback" layers.
- ASSISTIVE EXOSKELETONS — passive + powered load-bearing suits, active industrial/military industry.
  DOCUMENTED as deployed/dying-field research; MARKETING-speech guarded: no exoskeleton leaps or "enhanced
  soldier" claims in canon.
- PROSTHETICS — advanced bionic limbs + neural sensing. DOCUMENTED; heavily advertised — no "super-human arm
  recoil" claims.
- DEEP BRAIN STIMULATION (DBS) — implanted electrodes for therapy (movement/psychiatric indications).
  DOCUMENTED clinical reality; effect on mood/behavior is therapeutic-tuning, NOT personality upload.
- INTRACORTICAL ARRAYS (e.g., research-grade electrode arrays) — decode motor intent, experimental
  cursor/typing use. DOCUMENTED as research-stage. THE NEURAL-GRAMMARY that Shade's fiction tapers from.
- CLOSED-LOOP NEUROSTIM / responsive stimulation — stim following brain state via implanted sensing.
  DOCUMENTED early-clinical; fiction uses its "sensor→stimulator→sensation" loop shape, not its efficacy.
