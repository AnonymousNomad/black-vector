## Coastal/system species beats (for fiction use)

- BROWN/GRIZZLY-BEAR FISHING behavior: seasonal salmon-stream fishing, "bear-fishing towns," subadult-pressure
  pushing bears into camps — a real ecology driver for camp-guard warnings (BV-D043, D011/§16 human-wildlife
  boundary).
- RAVENS: real intelligence/kleptoparasitism, caching, "tidemark sentinel" behaviors — the fiction's dead-signal
  and camp-noise logic; ravens are a DOCUMENTED-adjacent wildlife trope but each individual behavior in-fiction
  is fictionalized.
- SEA OTTERS: keystone predator; real kelp/sea-urchin cascade; recovery from near-extirpation is DOCUMENTED —
  for the fiction: a species "coming back" changes shore ecology and camp practices (chain ≥ shore-use shifts).
- STELLER SEA LIONS: pinniped rookery dynamics, haul-out noise/scent — marine-system "sentries" and seasonal
  rookery anchors for the island's maritime/invasive logics.
- PACIFIC SALMON RETURN: seasonal biomass pulse; drives bear/predator/wharf cycles; the cannery and the isthmus
  seasons (ALASKA_SITES) all hinge on it — a system-clock for the world (D011/§4 regional system rules).
- MARINE MAMMAL & WHALE OBSERVATIONS: seasonal presence - used for shipping/travel pacing, not plot muscle.
- SCAVENGERS (eagles, gulls, foxes, wolverines): cleanup/nest ecology; foxes and wolverines in particular read
  "camp predator" and keep the never-fully-safe camp tone (BV-SKILL-018).

## Weather / season system (doctrine §30 // BV-D043 ally)
- Seasonal transitions are real gates in the fiction: freeze/late-season wharf death, blow-in flight windows,
  spring melt network fragility — wildlife and infrastructure lean on the SAME seasonal clock (ALASKA_SITES).

## Fiction plumbing notes
- Wildlife is a SYSTEM (food-pulse → predator → camp friction → character choice), never set-dressing.
- Real species names only as inspiration; any behavioral claim in canon is FICTIONALIZED DERIVATION unless
  explicitly DOCUMENTED (e.g., sea-otter recovery is DOCUMENTED; specific plot-behavior in-fiction is not).
- No wildlife "power = danger" inflation: danger scales from ecology, exhaustion, and terrain — not from
  magically aggravated fauna (keeps horror budget intact).
