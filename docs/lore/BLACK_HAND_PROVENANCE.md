## 2. 1930s — the original scientist

- **Dr. Helena Voss** (fictional, [PURE BLACK VECTOR FICTION]). American perceptual psychologist,
  trained in 1920s clinical psychology, peripheral to a small private neurological institute.
  Independently funded through a private medical trust.
- Genuinely attempting beneficial research: understanding attention, sleep, hypnosis, hemispheric
  behavior, unusual perception, "inattentive processing" and altered awareness — to help patients
  suffering from shell-shock-derived dissociation and chronic pain. Not trying to make weapons.
- Original hypothesis (period-appropriate, fictional): trained, stable attention might produce
  reproducible altered-perception states — projected attention, altered perception, subconscious
  information processing, unusual synchronized brain states. She called her approach the
  **"alignment work"**; surviving documents label it simply **VOSS**.
- NEVER called Hemi-Sync ([DISPUTED] Gateway in the ledger; the fiction refuses anachronism — the
  later institution borrows the structure and renames everything).
- Founding tragedy (canon): **the research was created to understand humanity and was eventually
  used to control it.** Voss dies before militaries ever reach her work; her recorded objection
  ("this was made for the wounded, not for armies") is preserved in one surviving file drawer —
  and ignored. Where she died, and whether her death was natural, is [UNRESOLVED, §24].

## 3. WWII — first militarization

- Mechanism: a wartime scientific-liaison office (fictional: the **"Liaison Desk for Technical
  Methods"**) reviewing clinical psychology literature for applied military value identifies VOSS.
  Voss herself is already dead; her papers and one living assistant are pulled in.
- Initial military interest is pragmatic: interrogation resistance, observation/spotter stability,
  cognitive performance under stress, survival decision-making under fatigue, information
  acquisition, unusual perception under sleep deprivation.
- The institution's first mistake (canon): **treating an unusual human phenomenon as a potential
  military capability.** No monsters, no telekinesis, no applied results — the wartime work produces
  "trained observation" methods indistinguishable from good fieldwork, plus a growing appetite for
  treating people as test beds.
- Wartime codeword continuity: **VOSS → "VEIL"** (a fabric/covering euphemism — administrative, not
