## 2. Design philosophy (frozen)

> **What does the player do, where do they go, what do they learn, how do they change, and why do they keep
> moving forward?**

The Season-1 spine is an ARRANGEMENT of existing systems into a journey. It is not a quest catalogue. It is not
a storyboard. It is a SPINE — the backbone the player walks along, where every layer the design has built
so far finds its place in time.

Frozen principles:
- The player keeps moving because the world keeps changing — what the player knows, what the player can do,
  what the world lets happen. The drive is internal + environmental + relational, not "next quest marker."
- Each layer's pacing is BOUNDED by the layer's own canonical scarcity (D011 §30 rarity, D014 knowledge-gate
  table, D015 §33 horror frequency, D016 §48 prevention matrix, D017 §3 reclamation stages). The spine respects
  these bounds; it does not pressure them into tighter cycles.
- ACT structure is not a hard three-act template. The directive allows the island topology + canon to
  dictate an honest structure. The name "Act" is a label for a band, not a genre.
- No "kill-XP-driven main story." Drive is composed of: (a) immediate survival need, (b) mystery curiosity
  about the island's truth, (c) reclamation — the felt change in the man, (d) relational care (ally, surviving
  teammates, communities), (e) the slow horror of certainty loss.

## 9. First undeniable anomalous event (frozen — recommendation per D018 §34)

Per D018 §34, propose 2–3 candidate placements and recommend one.

### Requirements (frozen, from D016 §11 + D018 §34)
- occurs after sufficient ambiguity (D016 Phase 0–1 closure)
- emotionally/physically consequential
- not "press psychic button to move crate"
- cannot reasonably be dismissed afterward
- does not make The Hand immediately proficient
- preferred structure: danger/desperation → involuntary impossible response → physiological cost → disbelief

### Candidates (CANDIDATE — D018 does NOT lock content)

**CANDIDATE A — The Cliff Latch (S-1 Strand edge; ACT II early)**
Context: The Hand is at a damaged structure edge above cold water, weapon gone or jammed, ordinary answer
exhausted (climb / fight / flee all close or failed). A dropped object — maybe the photograph — slides
toward the edge. He reaches. The object does what hands cannot: it stops, or it returns, in a way that
involves no visible lever, no wind, no muscle. The world reacts (sound, particle, a stillness, a witness if
present). The Hand's body pays (Strain band ELEVATED; tremor; brief audio narrowing; visible cold-sweat /
breath). The player and the Hand both know. The Hand immediately tries to dismiss it. The body does not let
him.

**CANDIDATE B — The Other Cell Door (Vivara-adjacent, ACT III mid)**
Context: The Hand is in a sealed program area (F4 Vivara descent or a tangent); a door that should require
force, equipment, or an ally's help SLAMS shut behind a threat, and the Hand, with hands at his side, stops
it. Or holds it. The cost is severe (Strain HIGH; involuntary shudder; a momentary perceptual distortion
that may be a Micro Bleed or may be Strain). The witness reads it. The threat's reaction freezes them.

**CANDIDATE C — The Drop and the Recovered (during ACT III Facility restoration)**
Context: During restoration work (F2 Tanellus medical or F3 Frostvane relay), a piece of equipment falls or
sits just out of reach in a place no human can reach it without a ladder, a tool, a partner, a delay. The
Hand reaches. It moves. He doesn't consciously try. His hands were at his sides.

### Recommendation (frozen)

**CANDIDATE A — The Cliff Latch (S-1 Strand edge; ACT II early).**

Reasoning (frozen):
- Sits after sufficient D016 Phase 0-1 ambiguity (early ACT II gives the player room to doubt the first
  ACT I micro-events).
- The PHOTOGRAPH as the trigger object preserves the doctrine §28 persistent-narrative-artifact law; the
  moment lands on the player's EMOTIONAL anchor, not on a system object.
- Emotionally/physically desperate circumstance (cliff edge, cold water, no good conventional answer).
- Involuntary response (he didn't try; he reached and it happened).
- Physiological cost (Strain band ELEVATED, tremor, breath) — but not CRITICAL, preserving room for Phase 2
  CONTROLLED EXPERIMENTATION (D016 §7).
- Indisputable (the world reacts; the photograph is in his hand; the Hand's body says so).
- DOES NOT make him proficient. He gains Phase 2 entry to deliberate experimentation, not a power.

Frozen rule: the bible does NOT lock the scene beyond the requirement. A future authored scene may use the
same shape, the same emotional anchor, and the same structural beats without copying the specific object or
location. The CONSTRAINT is the design contract, not the CONTENT.

## 20. Weather as campaign punctuation (frozen)

Per D018 §44 + D015 §14-§15.

Frozen rules:
- Major weather events are AUTHORED / FORECASTABLE / SEMI-AUTHORED — NOT random punishment (D015 §15).
- They can: close routes · enable infiltration under whiteout · enable preparation via forecast · alter coast

- Storm timing is forecasted through RESTORED weather instrumentation (F3 Frostvane, D015 §24) and local
  community knowledge (D015 §23).
- The player should be able to LEARN and PLAN around weather.

Frozen pacing role (campaign punctuation):
- ACT I: one MAJOR STORM that teaches the player weather-as-pressure. It closes a route or threatens an
  early shelter choice. The player survives the storm and learns the cost of unpreparedness.
- ACT II: STORMS that gate travel between F1 and F3, or between F1 and F2. They create forced pacing.
- ACT III: WHITE-ABLE BLIZZARD that enables infiltration of a program site (D015 §14 WHITEOUT).
- ACT IV: WEATHER-EXTREME convergence event — Storm that opens or closes access to F5 Black Hand Annex,
  combined with infrastructure failure (D015 §17-§21 propagation).

Frozen rule: weather NEVER carries the horror budget alone (D015 §14 / BV-SKILL-018). Weather + isolation +
darkness + cold can compose a horror moment, but the moment's authored event is in BV-SKILL-018.
