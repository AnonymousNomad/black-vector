## 2. Chronology: three distinct collapse/catastrophe referents

**RETAINED + OPERATOR-LOCKED.** These are editorial distinctions, not newly named in-world events.

1. **Personal/family collapse:** after HALF-LIGHT, homecoming and separation from the program's supply/logistics chain; deterioration, public killings and family deaths; before imprisonment. The public bloodscene and family catastrophe remain distinct beats (D014 §§16–17). Real killings; context does not absolve Corley (D028 §§7–8; D030 §8).
2. **Opening facility/containment catastrophe:** during island captivity, after covert diversion and experimentation; immediately precedes Strand awakening. D014 §25/D012 §19 retain island-interior expansion beyond containment as author context, without resolving Darkness metaphysics or certifying the cause in the opening. Corley does not know where she is, how long she was held, what happened, or why she survived.
3. **Late Season-1 surface catastrophe:** after campaign progression and Act IV convergence, Black Hand denial/failsafe action catastrophically damages/scuttles much of the surface operational infrastructure. Deep underground survives. Black Hand believes key subjects/assets destroyed; that belief is not their actual fate. This is not the opening event (§10).

Author chronology:

```text
HALF-LIGHT → extraction and identity reveal → homecoming/family life
→ supply separation and deterioration → public killings → family deaths
→ police/capture → conviction → prison → YEARS of transformation
→ subsequent prison killings → later capital conviction/death row
→ expected execution → covert diversion → captivity/nurse/fragmented experimentation
→ opening containment catastrophe → Strand awakening
→ Acts I–IV → separate late denial/failsafe surface catastrophe
→ living survivors in deep infrastructure → UNKNOWN HAND refuge/organizational seed
```

Nurse and experimentation scenes retain D014 §§23–24's captivity framing; exact interleaving is not a new fixed medical timeline. The photograph persists through these transitions, not as ordinary loot. Fragmented presentation must not erase the author timeline or tell the player all its causes.

**RETAINED:** D014 §§2–12,29,31. **OPERATOR-LOCKED:** last mission and reveal boundary.

Use the existing mission-phase vocabulary: **setup → infiltration → breach → crisis → extraction**. The historical operator is at her peak; the player receives approximately **60–70% of old-era conventional capability**, not the whole skill set and not the eventual anomaly ceiling. No overt paranormal display.

The audience sees timing and mutual familiarity, not the hidden biological explanation. No telepathy, implant network or future sight. The unit's bond is family; legacy BROTHERHOOD pillar/bond tokens do not redefine Corley or male-code the unit.

HALF-LIGHT may foreshadow loss through ordinary dramatic contrast. It must not require neurological failure at extraction or certify the later cause. Meaningful deterioration begins after supply-chain separation.

## 7. SECOND: mandatory confrontation, damaged prediction, persistent ally

**RETAINED + OPERATOR-LOCKED:** doctrine §§25–26; D018 §15; D028 §18; D029 §26; D030 §§14–17.

SECOND is **he/him**. He is neither psychic nor a seer. Exceptional pattern recognition, probability processing, movement/posture reading and predictive combat cognition coexist with damaged perception/reality integration. Others may reasonably think he has lost his mind because he reacts to patterns/threats they do not perceive. Some readings are correct, some distorted, some unresolved. Do not validate every suspicion or reduce him to an ordinary healthy coordinator.

**Placement:** late Act II / early Act III, after F1 stabilization and stronger memory evidence, before full program truth. Exact facility remains flexible; F5 core is not used to solve that staging problem.

Preserve the fixed scene, correcting protagonist pronouns rather than rewriting its outcome:

1. Corley explores a facility; SECOND fires three sudden shots. One hits **Corley's left shoulder**.
2. He closes immediately. She reaches for her boot knife; he overpowers/pins her against a wall and places a gun against her head.
3. Familiar old combat grammar and recognition matter within the confrontation; the worn Hidden Hand photograph falls into view.
4. SECOND recognizes it. Its condition/history establishes Corley's preservation of it through prison, death row and experimentation, challenging his certainty that she knowingly betrayed them.
5. He lowers the weapon. Emotional recognition and realization that she was deceived begin reconciliation.
6. SECOND becomes a **persistent allied character**, not optional recruitment or a lethal/nonlethal branch.

The photograph is identity/reality evidence, not universal persuasion. His trauma, history and damaged integration need not be completely resolved in Season 1. Persistent ally does not mean constant follower: selected operations, base support, analysis, reconnaissance and memory work remain bounded functions with his own limitations. No arbitrary stat aura or flawless reality oracle.

Casting is not character canon. Do not assign the operator's stepsister to SECOND or any other role without explicit confirmation.
