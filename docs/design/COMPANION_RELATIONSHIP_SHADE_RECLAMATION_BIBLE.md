## 4. Shade control architecture (frozen — D023 §1)

The Shade has:
- **Neural control implant** (the implant behind ear/skull interface; the channel for control commands)
- **Combat conditioning** (the trained-behavior overlay; what the Shade does when commanded)
- **Artificial behavioral restrictions** (the constraints the program imposes — what the Shade is
  FORBIDDEN to do without command)
- **Enhanced sensory processing** (the augmentation — D011 §6 TECHNICAL / EW family; legitimate
  augmented sensing, not supernatural)
- **Military training** (the operator-grade training — D011 §4 role families)

The control chain (frozen):
```
OPERATOR  ->  CONTROL GAUNTLET  ->  ENCRYPTED NEURAL COMMAND  ->  IMPLANT BEHIND EAR/SKULL INTERFACE  ->  SHADE NERVOUS SYSTEM OVERRIDE
```

- OPERATOR: the human operating the Shade (doctrine §26 + D011 §8 commander externalization + D022
  §5 squad command). The operator is a real person; the operator's choices drive the control commands.
- CONTROL GAUNTLET: the physical hardware through which the operator issues commands. The gauntlet
  communicates with the implant via encrypted neural commands. The gauntlet is a piece of equipment
  that can be damaged, recovered, or destroyed (D023 §6 first encounter).
- ENCRYPTED NEURAL COMMAND: the signal the operator issues. Encrypted; bypasses conscious thought; the
  Shade's nervous system executes the command WITHOUT conscious processing.
- IMPLANT BEHIND EAR/SKULL INTERFACE: the physical interface between the gauntlet and the Shade's
  nervous system. The implant is PART OF the Shade — it is not removable without significant damage.
- SHADE NERVOUS SYSTEM OVERRIDE: the override is what makes the Shade a weapon. The Shade's normal
  nervous system is suppressed; the override redirects voluntary action into commanded action.

The Shade is NOT a robot. The person is still inside.

### 4.1 The person's interior (frozen)

The Shade has an interior life that the override suppresses but does not destroy:
- The Shade experiences sensory input (the override does not blind or deafen; it redirects).
- The Shade has emotional response (the override redirects the response; it does not eliminate it).
- The Shade has memory (the override can suppress specific memories but the underlying storage is
  intact — D011 §6 BROKEN / DESYNCHRONIZED family + D012 §22 partial-information pattern).
- The Shade has preference (the override suppresses preference; D011 §9 says the Companion Shade is
  CAPABLE of eventual individual preference and refusal).

The horror is the GAP between the person's interior and the override. The person is still inside. The
armor is advanced. The weapon is real. The person is real. They are SEPARATE.

### 4.2 Override failure modes (frozen)

The override can fail in AUTHORED WAYS:
- **GAUNTLET DAMAGE**: the control chain breaks at the gauntlet; the Shade is no longer receiving
  commands; the Shade defaults to last-commanded behavior OR panics.
- **OPERATOR DEATH**: the control chain breaks at the operator; the gauntlet has no human input; the
  Shade defaults to last-commanded behavior OR panics.
- **IMPLANT DAMAGE**: the control chain breaks at the implant; the Shade's nervous system is partially
  freed; the Shade experiences the override's withdrawal as disorientation / pain / freedom.
- **COMMAND CONFLICT**: the override receives contradictory commands; the Shade's nervous system
  defaults to last-commanded behavior OR panics.
- **OVERRIDE FAILURE (program degradation)**: rare; the override degrades over time; the Shade
  gradually re-emerges. This is the D011 §6 BROKEN / DESYNCHRONIZED family.

D023 freezes the FIRST SHADE ENCOUNTER (§6) as a GAUNTLET DAMAGE + OPERATOR DEATH scenario — the
Shade the player meets is freed by the player's combat against the operator.

## 7. Shade relationship progression — five stages (frozen — D023 §1)

The Companion Shade's relationship with The Hand progresses through five stages:

```
Stage 1:  "Awaiting command."
Stage 2:  "Why did you spare me?"
Stage 3:  "I don't understand choice."
Stage 4:  "I am not your weapon."
Stage 5:  "I choose."
```

### 7.1 Stage 1 — "Awaiting command" (frozen)

- The Shade is freed by the gauntlet damage / operator death but has no override.
- The Shade defaults to last-commanded behavior OR panics.
- The Shade's interior life is fragmented; the Shade does not have language for "I am a person."
- The Shade's behavior is mechanical; the Shade is waiting for a command.

Player experience (frozen): the player encounters a damaged, confused human in advanced armor who
responds to commands. The player's choices begin to shape the relationship.

### 7.2 Stage 2 — "Why did you spare me?" (frozen)

- The Shade's first deliberate question.
- The Shade is learning that the player did not exploit the override's absence.
- The Shade is learning that the player CHOSE not to kill.
- The Shade does not understand the choice yet; the question is the first step.

Player experience (frozen): the player makes choices that teach the Shade about choice. The Shade
asks the question.

### 7.3 Stage 3 — "I don't understand choice." (frozen)

- The Shade is learning that choices are AVAILABLE.
- The Shade is learning that the player's choices have consequences for the Shade.
- The Shade is learning that the Shade CAN make choices.
- The Shade does not understand how to choose.

Player experience (frozen): the player presents choices to the Shade; the Shade attempts to make
them and fails; the player helps the Shade learn.

### 7.4 Stage 4 — "I am not your weapon." (frozen)

- The Shade rejects the player's attempts to use the Shade as a weapon.
- The Shade is learning that the Shade is not defined by what the Shade can do.
- The Shade is learning that the Shade is defined by what the Shade chooses.
- The Shade is becoming a person.

Player experience (frozen): the player has been treating the Shade as a person; the Shade begins
to recognize it.

### 7.5 Stage 5 — "I choose." (frozen)

- The Shade makes a deliberate choice that the Shade did not have to make.
- The choice is not commanded; the choice is not trained; the choice is the Shade's own.
- The Shade has become a person.

Player experience (frozen): the player witnesses the Shade's first authentic choice. The Companion
arc reaches its first completion.

### 7.6 Progression pacing (frozen)

The 5 stages are NOT linear. They are iterative. The Shade may cycle through stages (especially
between Stage 2 and Stage 3). The player must be patient.

The progression is anchored to ACT IV (D018 §13) for introduction, with the full arc extending
through the late game. D023 does NOT lock stage timing — scene-authoring directives will.
