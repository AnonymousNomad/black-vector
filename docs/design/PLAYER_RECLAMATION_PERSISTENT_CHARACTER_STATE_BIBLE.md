## 2. Governing principle (frozen)

> **The player should not merely unlock a stronger Hand. They should watch and feel a broken man gradually
> rebuild himself into something neither prison nor Hidden Hand originally produced.**

This is the master design law. Progression is RECLAMATION — the recovery, recombination, and rewriting of a man.
It is not "level up." It is not "skill tree says +10% competence." The Hand does not become what he was; he
becomes a third thing, made of prison, the program, the island, the player's choices, and the things he kept and
the things he let go.

## 3. Reclamation arc — five stages (frozen, canonical)

D017 extends doctrine §38 (BV-D040) with a prologue-aware stage 0 and a STAGE 1 (BROKEN SURVIVOR) bridge. Each
stage is a NARRATIVE-PLAYABLE STATE, not a number on a meter.

### STAGE 0 — LEGEND (prologue only)
The Hand at OPERATIONAL PEAK: controlled · disciplined · integrated equipment · team support · precision
identity. This is the REFERENCE POINT, not the starting state of the main game. Seen only in the prologue
(D014 §1, §29). The player witnesses what was, in order to understand what is being rebuilt and why it costs.

### STAGE 1 — BROKEN SURVIVOR (main-game opening)
The Hand is: deconditioned · prison-hardened · physically degraded · poorly equipped · angry · suspicious ·
improvisational · memory-fragmented · disconnected from old operator habits. He is still dangerous. He is not
incompetent. Doctrine §4 starting lock is intact. His prison instincts are sharper than his operator instincts;
his rifle is gone; his team is gone; the photograph is what he has.

This stage bridges BV-D040's BROKEN/PRISONER band to the playable substrate (D015 survival state architecture).

### STAGE 2 — RELEARNING
Old procedural capability begins resurfacing (doctrine §3 memory sequence; BV-SKILL-012). The player recognizes:
weapon familiarity · fieldcraft · situational awareness · disciplined movement · precision habits · old tactical
instincts. Progression moments appear BEFORE narrative explanation: a moment of cleaner reload animation, an
instinctive angle check, a body position the operator takes without being told.

### STAGE 3 — RECOVERING OPERATOR
Military capability and island-survivor capability begin MERGING. The Hand stops simply remembering his old self;
he begins CHOOSING what to keep. Visual evolution reflects this (D017 §12 law; §35 animation-evolution states).
Procedural choices (grooming, gear, repair decisions) start to feel like authorship, not maintenance.

### STAGE 4 — INTEGRATED HAND (late Season 1)
Combines: old Hidden Hand discipline · prison adaptability · Simse Sound survival knowledge · restored Control
(doctrine §36 / BV-D035) · anomalous integration (D016 §49 ceiling) · player-shaped equipment/style. He is NOT a
reset version of the prologue character. He is a new synthesis. Doctrine §37.3 alignment preserved: the late-game
Hand becomes something the original Hidden Hand operator never was.

Frozen rule: STAGE 4 still fundamentally requires firearms · knives · CQC · stealth · cover · planning · allies
· shelter · medical care (D016 §49; if reclamation makes those obsolete, design has failed).

## 4. Reclamation law (frozen)

Progression must not mean "skill tree says +10% competence." Use multiple PROGRESSION CHANNELS, each progressing
differently:

```
PHYSICAL RECOVERY          — endurance, coordination, strength, mobility, fine motor, pain tolerance, recovery
PROCEDURAL MEMORY          — doctrine §3 sequence; old skills resurfacing diegetically
PLAYER PRACTICE            — repetition/refinement, diegetic rehearsal not stat-grind
EQUIPMENT ACCESS           — weapons, gear, armor, tools, base facilities
ENVIRONMENTAL MASTERY      — D015 substrate competence; the player learns to PREPARE, not to be immune
CONTROL / SELF-MASTERY     — doctrine §36; volatility reduced through practice and path
ANOMALOUS DISCOVERY        — D016 five-phase progression; reclamation + power co-evolve
RELATIONSHIPS              — ally (doctrine §25–§26), community (D015 §22), surviving teammates
COMPOUND / INDEPENDENT     — doctrine §37 paths; progression routes alter recovery profile
VISUAL EVOLUTION           — the persistent character-state channel this bible authors
```

Each channel has its OWN cadence. Some move quickly (procedural memory, environmental mastery). Some move slowly
(physical recovery, anomalous discovery). The player feels the difference.

## 5. No XP-for-everything (frozen — recommendation)

Question: does BLACK VECTOR need conventional XP?

Recommendation (frozen): **NO conventional XP.** The progression model is memory + use + discovery + story gate
+ training + equipment + recovery + mastery. Free kill-XP → buy-unrelated-skill chains are FORBIDDEN.

Rationale (frozen):
- Doctrine §3 forbids generic XP trees, levels, skill points, arbitrary stat progression.
- The Hand's identity is not a class; progression is diegetic memory + reclaim.
- BV-D040 lists mechanical progressors as TIMING TOLERANCE / RECOVERY DISCIPLINE / STAMINA ECONOMY / THREAT
  AWARENESS / TRANSITIONS / RESTRAINT / POSITIONAL CONTROL / CONTROL RECOVERY / TK PRECISION — none is a generic
  number.

ALLOW (frozen): a lightweight in-fiction currency MAY exist IF justified for in-world purchases (workshop fees,
trade, base repair consumables), but it is NOT the primary progression driver. It is a tool of the world, not a
meter of the player. The currency's existence is CANDIDATE; if a future directive authors it, it must trace to a
specific in-world economy (not a kill-reward funnel).

## 6. Progression categories (frozen)

Bound the categories. The player's progression lives across them; the player does NOT select classes.

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| OPERATOR | marksmanship familiarity; tactical observation; weapon handling; fieldcraft; stealth discipline; overwatch knowledge |
| SURVIVOR | cold/weather adaptation; shelter use; navigation; improvised equipment; environmental reading |
| FIGHTER | CQC; stance/control; prison-derived dirty survival habits; old military technique integration |
| MIND | Control; emotional regulation; resistance to distortion; memory integration |
| ANOMALOUS | D016-owned; D017 does NOT recreate its capability tree |
| EQUIPMENT | armor; weapons; clothing; tools; base facilities |

Recommendation (frozen): these should remain **UNDER-THE-HOOD** as authoring categories. The player sees
reclamation stage (STAGE 0–4) and channel-specific FEEDBACK (a cleaner reload animation, a wetter clothing
decal, a steadier grip on the rifle). The player does NOT see a category dashboard or a "+1 Operator" popup.
Category labels live in the design document, not the HUD.

## 7. Procedural memory (frozen — D017 extends doctrine §3 / BV-SKILL-012)

The Hand should sometimes know things BEFORE consciously remembering why:

- instinctive rifle handling
- checking angles without prompting
- equipment familiarity that surprises him
- route selection that turns out correct
- body positioning during a transition
- recognizing military systems at a glance

Implementation law (frozen):
- Progression moments appear as subtle animation/gameplay efficiency BEFORE narrative explanation.
- The hand moves correctly first; the memory lands second.
- Memory recovery is NOT a download — it is a slow surfacing with cost (strain, attention, recognition, body
  echo).
- The animation gets slightly cleaner; the timing tightens slightly; the player feels it before they read it.

This binds with the D017 §35 animation-evolution states (BROKEN → REMEMBERING → RECOVERING → INTEGRATED).

## 8. Recovered / Learned / Integrated distinction (frozen, mandatory)

The Hand's capabilities must be classified into three categories — not three trees, three ORIGINS:

| Origin | Reads as | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| RECOVERED | Things he knew before imprisonment | Hidden Hand discipline, sniper habits, perimeter routines, equipment fluency, operational restraint |
| LEARNED | Things developed through prison / Simse Sound / survival / new relationships | prison instincts, improvised weapon use, weather reading, community trust reads, anomalous capability diegetic surfacing |
| INTEGRATED | Abilities ONLY possible because old and new combine | operator+survivor CQC, anomalous-assisted weapon recall, layered gear combining HH discipline + island scrounge, late-game restraint from prior rage-control work |

Frozen rule: every reclamation-stage gain is tagged at design time with one (or more) of these origins. The
player does NOT see the tag; the design uses it to keep reclamation honest. A pure RECOVERED gain that
mirrors STAGE 0 1:1 is a violation — it would imply restoration, not reclamation.

## 9. Physical recovery (frozen — modeled, not stat-grinded)

The Hand begins significantly degraded. Recovery must address (broad recovery dimensions, frozen list):

```
endurance · coordination · strength · mobility · fine motor control · pain tolerance · recovery rate
```

- NO bodybuilding stat grind. No numeric bars to push.
- Numbers hidden or minimal unless useful (a player-readable band may be exposed later, CANDIDATE).
- Recovery is gated by D015 substrate: rest in a HEATED STRUCTURE with sleep and food beats moves recovery faster
  (D015 §6, §12, §22); cold/wetness/exhaustion moves it slower; injury narrows it.
- The body's RECOVERY is a diegetic event: the player feels cleaner movement before they read it.
- A physical dimension may NEVER return to STAGE 0 level (some scar, some limit, some chronic effect persists
  permanently). What persists is authored, not random.# — CONTINUED: PART B — PERSISTENT STATE LAW, HAIR/GROOMING ARCHITECTURE —

## 10. Persistent character visual state — major Season-1 system (frozen)

**Governing law (frozen):** **THE CHARACTER MODEL IS A LIVING SAVE FILE.** The player's history should
become visible on The Hand. Every change persists until an actual system changes it; no arbitrary reset.

The Hand's appearance should continuously communicate "what has happened to him." This is the AUTHORING CHANNEL
that bridges D017 reclamation law with BV-SKILL-022 equipment visual language. Frozen STATE CHANNELS:

| Channel | What it carries |
|---|---|
| BODY | wounds · bruising · scars · fatigue · cold response · blood · grime |
| HAIR / FACE | hair growth · beard/stubble growth · wetness · dirt · grooming · sleep deprivation · healing |
| CLOTHING | wetness · dirt · tears · repairs · layering · replacement |
| ARMOR | scratches · impact damage · dents · repairs · substituted pieces · attachment changes |
| WEAPONS | wear · damage state · residue · repaired condition · provenance markers |
| ENVIRONMENTAL RESIDUE | snow · rain · salt · mud · ash · blood |

Frozen law: these channels SHARE GROUND TRUTH with D015 survival substrate (wetness/cold/blood/exposure) and
with BV-SKILL-022 equipment visual state. No channel reinvents the data. The state architecture is the system;
the visual representation is its expression.

## 11. Visual state must persist (frozen, system law)

> **If something changes, it should remain changed until an actual system changes it.**

Examples (frozen set):
- Shaving persists until hair grows.
- Clothing remains wet until dried.
- A repaired tear remains visibly repaired.
- A scar remains.
- Armor damage remains until repaired/replaced.
- Mud remains until washed/worn away.
- Beard grows over time.

Frozen rule: **NO arbitrary visual resets between scenes.** A scene boundary, save/load, or chapter transition
is NOT an excuse to reset visible state. State continuity is a design law; the implementation must enforce it.

## 12. Visible state → physical consequence → natural response (frozen, system law)

> **If a physical state is visible, evaluate whether it should have a small believable consequence and whether
> the player has a natural response to it.**

Examples (frozen set):

| Visible state | Consequence | Response |
|---|---|---|
| Loose long hair | occasional sightline/headgear interference | brush aside / tie back |
| Wet clothing | faster thermal loss | shelter / heat / dry/change |
| Dirty goggles | reduced clarity | wipe/clean |
| Damaged shoulder | altered weapon posture | treatment/rest/adaptation |
| Mud-caked boots | weight + reduced insulation at seams | shake off / change / accept |
| Blood on hands | social reaction triggers (community, NPC reads) | wash / ignore / accept consequences |

Frozen rules:
- NOT every visible condition needs a gameplay penalty. Some are expressive (they communicate the man's life)
  without changing numbers.
- USE JUDGMENT. A consequence must be SMALL, BELIEVABLE, and RESPONSIVE. A penalty must be removable through
  NORMAL PLAY (not maintenance spam, D017 §13).
- The response layer is diegetic — animations, brush-aside gestures, tool use, base interactions. No mandatory
  sub-menus for routine responses.
- A consequence that has no natural response is over-penalized and should be reduced or removed.
- A visible state that has no consequence AND no response is decoration (acceptable for flavor, not for a
  system).

## 13. Anti-tedium law (frozen)

> **Persistent realism must never become maintenance spam.**

The player should occasionally think: "That physically makes sense."

Not: "I have twelve chores before I can play."

Frozen rules:
- High-frequency maintenance tasks (grooming ticks every in-game hour, dirt alerts that spam) are FORBIDDEN.
- Recovery happens at SUBSTRATE CADENCE (D015), not at UI cadence. The player perceives it through visible
  state changes and gameplay efficiency, not through nudges.
- A grooming or maintenance action that does not produce a meaningful change is cut.
- Personal-presentation items (hair ties, brushes) must be EASY TO ACQUIRE (the world has them) and SIMPLE TO USE.

## 14. Hair system (frozen)

The Hand's long hair is a functional visual state. Frozen states:

```
LOOSE · LOOSELY TIED · PONYTAIL/TIED BACK · UNDER HOOD · CONSTRAINED BY HEADGEAR · WET/MATTED
```

Frozen rules:
- Hair growth progresses over MEANINGFUL TIME SPANS (in-fiction days), not minutes.
- Do not simulate individual strands as gameplay objects.
- Use bounded visual/animation states — finite hair meshes / states, not a strand-by-strand system (D017 §50).
- Hair state is a PLAYER-AUTHORED PRESENTATION CHOICE (D017 §17 ties / §18 radial / §20 grooming).

## 15. Functional hair consequences (frozen)

Loose long hair may occasionally:

- enter peripheral view
- blow across first-person / precision view
- interfere visually with scope alignment
- cling when wet
- interact awkwardly with some headgear

Frozen rules:
- Do NOT apply numerical accuracy penalties merely because hair is loose.
- Prefer actual VISUAL / ANIMATION consequences (sightline obstruction, scope view partial block, wind catching
  hair — read on the body, not on the numbers).
- Hair consequences are NOT a combat handicap. Hair is expression + small realism, not a stat.

## 16. Hair response (frozen)

Possible natural responses:
- automatic brief brush-aside animation (player or AI character animation; situational)
- manual brush-aside (an action the player can take; not a chore)
- tie back (radial / base interaction)
- use bandanna
- use headgear

Frozen rules:
- Hair tied back should SUBSTANTIALLY REDUCE obstruction. This must remain a subtle realism system.
- An automatic brush-aside should happen for the cases that are clearly transient (wind catching hair once);
  for sustained obstruction the player should choose to tie back.
- A character-event beat (looking into a mirror, a moment of reflection) can include a hair-adjustment action
  that is not a chore — it is characterization (D017 §40 mirror moments).

## 17. Hair ties / bands (frozen, design recommendation)

Hair ties, rubber bands, improvised strips, and suitable bandannas may exist as low-value functional items.

Recommendation (frozen): **one reusable generic hair-tie item** with a small inventory stack (5–10 typical), plus
optional bandanna as a presentation accessory. Avoid durability simulation unless justified. Do not create loot
clutter.

Frozen rules:
- No "rare" hair tie. No "epic" bandanna. These are tools of the world, not collectibles.
- The item should be acquirable in-fiction at general stores, survivor caches, civilian homes, and base stores
  without contrived scarcity.
- A presentation accessory (bandanna) may also function as a basic face covering or signal marker; this is a
  world-meaningful use, not a stat.# — CONTINUED: PART C — RADIAL, BASE GROOMING, CLEANLINESS, BLOOD, RESIDUE, CLOTHING, ARMOR —

## 18. Quick presentation radial (frozen, recommendation)

Evaluate a held D-pad direction opening a compact appearance/presentation radial.

Candidate quick actions:

```
TIE HAIR · RELEASE HAIR · HOOD UP/DOWN · FACE COVERING UP/DOWN · GOGGLES POSITION · HELMET ON/OFF (where allowed)
```

Requirements (frozen):
- fast
- contextual
- no deep menu
- usable with controller and tablet
- state persists (D017 §11)
- unavailable options clearly explained (e.g., helmet requires equipped headgear; tie-back requires hair length)

Frozen rules:
- Maximum slot count: **6** (D017 §18 recommendation). Beyond 6 becomes a chore surface.
- The radial does NOT replace base-grooming interfaces (D017 §19). It is the QUICK layer; the base is the
  DELIBERATE layer.
- Touch input on tablet is mapped to the same actions via on-screen contextual cues; no nested touch menus for
  frequent actions.

## 19. Base grooming (frozen, system law)

Once The Hand establishes a functioning safe base (doctrine §31; D015 §24; BV-SKILL-020), contextual facilities
allow deliberate grooming. Frozen facility types and allowed actions:

| Facility | Actions |
|---|---|
| MIRROR / BATHROOM | shave · beard trim · wash face · select hair arrangement · inspect visible injuries/scars |
| WASH / DRY AREA | clean clothing · dry clothing |
| LOCKER | clothing · armor · stored presentation states |
| WORKBENCH | armor repair · equipment repair · weapon condition work (game abstraction, D017 §29) |
| MEDICAL AREA | inspect/treat injuries (D015 §13 treatment-rich decisions; this is not a hospital) |

Frozen rules:
- The base is NOT a life simulator. Each facility exists because a player action makes it useful (restore heat
  to power it, choose to spend time, etc.). A facility that exists only as decoration is a design smell (BV-SKILL-
  022 expectation doctrine; D015 §16 infrastructure consequence law).
- The base reflects RECOVERY (D017 §39). The more reclaimed the operator, the more deliberate the base becomes;
  but it never crosses into base-building-strategy.
- A grooming or treatment action the player takes is CHARACTERIZATION (D017 §20) plus mechanical effect. The
  facility surface mediates both.

## 20. Grooming as characterization (frozen)

Appearance decisions should express the player's interpretation of The Hand. Frozen trajectory options:

```
- maintain rough prison-survivor appearance
- gradually reconstruct old operator appearance
- create a hybrid identity
- deliberately abandon the old Hidden Hand look
```

Frozen rules:
- No morality score on grooming choices. A shaved head is not "good"; a long beard is not "bad."
- No canonical "correct haircut."
- An authored player-presentation outcome preserves The Hand's core recognizable identity (D017 §54 character

## 22. Cleanliness (frozen)

Dirt / blood / grime matters VISUALLY. Potential small consequences:

```
- social reactions (community reads)
- scent / animal implications only if JUSTIFIED (D015 wildlife informational reads)
- comfort (diegetic; expressed through animation, not a stat)
- equipment visibility (blood/grime on goggles reduces clarity — D017 §12 example)

```

Frozen rules:
- No hygiene meter.
- Washing is satisfying, not mandatory busywork.
- Consequences are SMALL and RESPONSIVE (D017 §12 law). A clean character reads differently to the world, and

## 23. Blood (frozen)

Blood can persist temporarily on:

```
SKIN · CLOTHING · ARMOR · WEAPONS
```

Frozen rules:
- Context matters (D017 §12). Civilian / community reactions may differ when The Hand enters covered in blood.
- Do NOT make gore accumulation cartoonishly extreme.
- Blood is washed, dried, flaked, and fades — visual cycles, not a UI timer.
- A weapon with blood residue reads as USED. The residue is communication; the player sees the cost of the
  kill (Pillar 3 — RESPONSIBILITY, doctrine §34).

## 24. Wetness visualization (frozen — compatibility with D015)

D015 owns wetness MECHANICALLY (D015 §10). D017 owns CHARACTER-STATE PRESENTATION COMPATIBILITY.

Representation (frozen):
- fabric darkening (material parameter; cheap on tablet)
- water sheen (subtle specular response)
- dripping (low-cost animation layer or shader)
- hair state (clinging when wet; D017 §15 consequence)
- breath / cold response (doctrine §30 / D015 §9 thermal bands)

Frozen rules:
- No need for expensive full-material simulation everywhere.
- Design tablet-feasible representation (D017 §50 architecture).
- Wet clothing dries over time in a HEATED STRUCTURE (D015 §12) or a fire (D015 §20). Visible state persists
  until the system changes it (D017 §11).

## 25. Snow / mud / salt (frozen — environmental residue)

Environmental residue should accumulate selectively. Frozen zones:

```
BOOTS / LOWER LEGS · KNEES · GLOVES · WEAPON CONTACT POINTS · OUTER ARMOR · HAIR / SHOULDERS
```

Frozen rules:
- Use masks / decals / material parameters rather than duplicate full character assets (D017 §50).
- Selective accumulation (boots first; shoulders last) communicates the player's movement and exposure without

- Residue is cleaned by environment (rain, water), time, deliberate wash, or wear-off through movement. No

## 30. Signature equipment return (frozen — D017 design moment)

The player should EVENTUALLY encounter equipment resembling The Hand's former precision identity. This should
create RECOGNITION BEFORE EXPOSITION.

Potential signals (frozen set):
- altered handling animation (the hand knows the weapon)
- hesitation (a moment of pause)
- procedural familiarity (a reload the operator doesn't have to think about)
- memory flash (a brief perception; aligned with D015 §31 Micro Bleed)
- special inspection (the player looks at the item longer than usual; an inspection screen that shows detail)

Frozen rules:
- Do NOT pop a system message: "Sniper skill unlocked."
- The moment is diegetic: the player's body moves, the camera lingers, the air is different. The skill tree
  hears nothing.
- The signature return is an authored EVENT, not an ambient reward.

## 31. Equipment attachment (frozen)

Allow some equipment to gain emotional/player value.

Frozen rules:
- Do NOT assume higher-numbered gear automatically replaces older gear.
- Repairability, familiarity, and history can preserve relevance.
- A piece the player has carried across sectors carries weight beyond its stats (diegetic; not a stat).
- Equipment loss is a STORY moment (D017 §46 permanent consequences — when authored).

## 32. Control progression (frozen — D017 extensions, BV-D035/D036/D037 preserved)

D008/D009 own Control mechanics. D017 specifies PROGRESSION MEANING:

| Stage | Control reads as |
|---|---|
| Early (STAGE 1–2) | Control is UNSTABLE and REACTIVE; rage and tunnel vision intrude; the operator fights them as much as the enemy |
| Mid (STAGE 2–3) | player learns DELIBERATE REGULATION; tension between discipline and reactivity becomes a player choice (lower Control = commitment / less hesitation; higher Control = restraint / better windows) |
