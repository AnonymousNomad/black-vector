# BLACK VECTOR — PROJECT DOCTRINE

> Status: CANONICAL — Governing. Reconciled under Directives 002 and 003.
> This file records the permanent project constraints and design doctrine for BLACK VECTOR.
> It is the reference the Developer's Way, the SOPs, and the domain skills all serve.

## 1. Identity

BLACK VECTOR is a separate project from NOMADIC CREED — an independent codebase, its own engine configuration,
its own canon, its own implementation directives, and its own project structure. Nothing from NOMADIC CREED is
carried over unless a directive explicitly says so.

- Genre: private, edge-device-first THIRD-PERSON SURVIVAL / STEALTH / TACTICAL-ACTION / PSYCHOLOGICAL-HORROR game.
- Engine: Godot 4.x, GDScript-first (no Mono/C# on the Android editor toolchain).
- Primary platform: Android tablet; performance-conscious sector architecture; touch-first input. Desktop is a later port target, never the design center.
- Local-first: fully offline, no cloud services, no telemetry, no accounts, no DRM.
- Single persistent world (one world, one continuous space) — a large fictional Alaska-inspired coastal wilderness region.
- Systemic scope: deliberately SMALLER than NOMADIC CREED. Fewer systems, deeper execution.
- OBJECTIVE (fixed): «High perceived depth, atmosphere and immersion from a bounded number of interacting systems.»
- BLACK VECTOR is NOT a universal military simulation platform. It is NOT a conventional mission-lobby shooter.

## 2. Product Pillars

1. KNOWLEDGE — There is no global truth. Every piece of knowledge in the world has a source, a channel, and a degree of reliability. This now includes the protagonist's OWN memory: recollections may be conditioned, damaged, or manipulated — his memory is a source with damaged reliability, never an omniscient narrator.
2. SIGNATURE — Nothing the player does is binary-on/off. The world senses the player through distinct, detectable signature
   channels (visual, movement, silhouette, illumination, acoustic, environmental, thermal, electronic, social — and more to be
   designed). There is no `hidden = true`. Concealment is a reduction of detectability across channels, never an integer flag.
3. CONSEQUENCE — Actions produce causal, remembered effects. The world has memory and reacts coherently. This includes moral
   consequence: the Hand committed real acts and the world (and his conscience) does not absolve him.

## 3. Narrative / Progression

Progression is MEMORY, not XP.

- Player fantasy: the Hand — «convicted murderer awaiting execution» who was secretly a conditioned, exploited operator — recovering who he was while surviving a wilderness he does not understand (region) and a past he cannot trust.
- Sequence: EXPERIENCE → TRIGGER → MEMORY → RECOGNITION → CAPABILITY RESTORED.
- Triggers: a place, object, weapon, combat situation, person, injury, sound, landmark, or recovered information.
- The prologue establishes the Hand at OPERATIONAL PEAK; later gameplay shows those abilities lost, fragmented, and selectively recovered (doctrine §29).
- Memory is also PERIL: amnesia, memory fragmentation, missing time, conditioning disruption, and psionic-cost memory/perception events are mechanics and narrative, not only flavor (doctrine §22).
- CAPABILITY FAMILIES ("disciplines") — recovered families, NOT eight huge RPG trees: FIELDCRAFT, RECON, INFILTRATION, CLOSE QUARTERS, MARKSMAN, ASSAULT, TECH, PSIONICS (doctrine §32).
- Generic XP trees, levels, skill points, and arbitrary stat progression are FORBIDDEN.
- The HIDDEN HAND PHOTOGRAPH is the anchor object of the whole history — a persistent narrative artifact (doctrine §28), never disposable inventory.

## 4. Starting Character Lock

At wilderness start, the Hand has very little:

- Compact sidearm (handgun) + 1 loaded magazine + 1 spare magazine.
- 1 fixed-blade boot knife.
- The Hidden Hand photograph (persistent narrative artifact, not loot).
- NO rifle, NO carbine, NO large arsenal, no conventional power fantasy at start.
- Direct combat must exist but must never be the universal optimal solution. Early identity is a broken man in a hostile world with dangerous instincts.

## 5. Environment Doctrine

- The world is a large fictional Alaska-inspired coastal wilderness region: boreal forest, mountains, snow, fjords/coast, islands, abandoned roads, isolated settlements, military infrastructure, research facilities, underground complexes, severe weather.
- The world is CONTINUOUS. Internally it is divided into independently activatable SECTORS so only the player's relevant region receives expensive simulation (doctrine §31). The player must not perceive conventional level selection.
- The world ITSELF is the game's mission space, progression space, survival space, exploration interface, and narrative delivery mechanism. Avoid unnecessary lobby screens and mission-selection infrastructure.
- Technical loading may exist. When practical, CONCEAL it through: traversal, interiors, weather, boat travel, caves, tunnels, elevators, distance, and sector transitions. (No streaming implemented in methodology phase.)
- Terrain is gameplay. Every environment surface is a candidate for traversal, concealment, positioning, observation, escape, or ambush.
- Trees are EXPLICIT gameplay terrain, with a concrete required arc:
  CLIMB → CONCEAL → OBSERVE → SEARCH → HIDDEN → CONTEXTUAL DROP ASSASSINATION (future).
- Governing rule: "If the player reasonably expects terrain to be usable for traversal, concealment, positioning, observation, escape, or ambush, BLACK VECTOR should support that expectation wherever technically practical."

## 6. Stealth Doctrine

- Stealth is multi-channel signature reduction, never `hidden = true`.
- Do NOT build an unnecessarily expensive military sensor simulator. Perception must be computationally bounded, simulation-cheap, and legible.
- The player must be able to understand and predict the senses of the world from evidence (visual, audio, and behavioral cues), not from debug internals.
- ENVIRONMENTAL channel includes Alaska weather: storms, snow, noise-masking and visibility collapse are legitimately usable, designed mechanics — and they are also survival hazards (doctrine §30).
- Hunter/prey: wildlife sensing follows the same bounded, signature-based rules the human perception uses.

## 7. Assassination Doctrine (present and future)

Two distinct positional states:

- UNSEEN → POSITION → OPPORTUNITY → ASSASSINATION
- DETECTED → ENGAGEMENT → EXCHANGE → (COUNTER | EVADE | BREAK | DISENGAGE) → ADVANTAGE → FINISH | ESCAPE

Assassination and combat are distinct states of play with distinct input, camera, and consequence, not the same loop.

## 8. Close-Combat Doctrine

Close-combat conceptual states:

READ → ENGAGE → EXCHANGE → (DEFLECT | EVADE | COUNTER | BREAK | DISENGAGE) → ADVANTAGE → FINISH | ESCAPE

- Influence is conceptual only: directional/read-based exchanges, timed counters/deflections, heavy impact, contextual assassination, tactical close quarters.
- NO enormous combo lists. Do not build NOMADIC CREED's entire combat ontology.
- No system implements the vocabulary under the methodology phase.
- ARCHITECTURE AUTHORITY (Directive 008): the frozen battle-state/range model — ADVANTAGE → CONTROL → RESOLUTION → RESET replaces FINISH | ESCAPE as the canonical after-initiative flow, plus Control/Neural Strain, the early-game Hand/rage framing, and the AI-facing combat interfaces — is defined in doctrine §35-§39. This section remains the compact vocabulary reference.

## 9. Weapon Doctrine

- Weapons are tools with tradeoffs (mass, handling, stability, capacity, projectile, recoil, optics, report/signature, mobility). No rarity tiers, no arbitrary RPG damage numbers.
- Hunting, fishing, and primitive/utility implements (traps, fire starters, shelter tools) are gameplay-relevant later — tools of survival, not an arsenal (doctrine §30).
- The starting lock (Section 4) governs the opening state. Rifles/carbines are recovered capability, gated by memory/disciplines (doctrine §3), never the open.

## 10. Platform and Renderer Decision

Recorded as a decision with evidence, not a lock. Revisit at first device profile.

- DEPENDENCY NOTE (fact, not carry-over): the on-device Android editor requires an OpenGL-compatible rendering
  path; Forward+ is not recommended on the Android editor due to severe performance limits.
- Godot 4 provides three renderers: Forward+ (desktop, Vulkan/D3D12/Metal, all features),
  Mobile (Vulkan/D3D12/Metal, half-float precision → strong tablet performance), and
  GL Compatibility (OpenGL, widest driver reach, no compute shaders, no normal/roughness buffer,
  no VRS/DOF/decals, depth pre-pass enabled by default).
- DEFAULT DECISION (BV-D001): target **GL Compatibility** as the initial rendering method — widest tablet driver
  support, simplest correctness contract, and the path the Android editing toolchain is proven against.
- UPGRADE GATE (documented trigger, not a promise): if a device profile shows headroom AND visual/feature
  requirements force it, evaluate the Mobile renderer under SOP-004 budgets with device evidence.
  Forward+ remains FORBIDDEN for Android targets.
- Android editor facts (see skill `godot-android-edge`): Android 5+ / OpenGL 3+; no C#/Mono; no external script
  editors; GABE companion app enables on-device Gradle APK/AAB export; all-files permission needed;
  Android 15+ 16 KB page support in Godot 4.5.
- GDExtension plugins are supported on the Android editor. GDScript is first-class there.

## 11. Scope Doctrine

- Systemic scope is intentionally smaller than NOMADIC CREED. Fewer systems; each one deep, legible, and consequential.
- Complexity is spent where the player perceives it (signature, consequence, environment, horror, survival decisions) — not on invisible bookkeeping.
- A system that cannot be made simulation-cheap and legible is redesigned or cut, not shipped opaque.
- Survival is BOUNDED by design: it must create decisions, not constant meter maintenance and busywork (doctrine §30).
- BLACK VECTOR is not a universal military simulation and not a mission-lobby shooter (doctrine §1). Feature requests that push toward either are rejected as scope violations.

## 12. Hierarchy and Authority

THE DEVELOPER'S WAY → BLACK VECTOR PROJECT DOCTRINE → ENGINEERING/EXECUTION SOPs → DOMAIN SKILLS → CURRENT TASK DIRECTIVE → IMPLEMENTATION.

- Lower layers may specialize, never silently override.
- If a task directive conflicts with this doctrine or the Developer's Way, STOP and report the conflict. Do not improvise.

## 14. Protagonist — The Hand

The playable protagonist is referred to historically as THE HAND.

- He was an honorable soldier: highly disciplined, deeply loyal, respected by subordinates, trusted by people whose lives he saved.
- Founder and commander of the Hidden Hand unit; its prime operational subject WITHOUT his knowledge.
- He was not originally a villain.
- His greatest vulnerability was his trust in the institution he served.

## 15. The Hidden Hand Unit

THE HIDDEN HAND is the UNIT (not the protagonist's individual callsign).

- An exceptionally small covert special-action unit. The Hand helped select its members, trained them, specialized them, commanded them, and regarded them as family — becoming the central reason they trusted the mission.
- The unit believed it was conducting compartmented national-security operations at the highest level.
- Unit values: precision, service, loyalty, protecting teammates, protecting civilians where possible, mission discipline, minimal operational footprint.
- Its members were genuinely brothers. That brotherhood was real even if the institution controlling them was corrupt.

## 16. The Secret Military Program (fictional)

The Hidden Hand did not knowingly volunteer for chemical enhancement.

- For approximately years of service (potentially 10-20 for the longest-exposed), routine logistics secretly contained an experimental FICTIONAL compound/regimen. Vectors inside the fiction: rations, supplements, hydration products, recovery compounds, routine medical logistics.
- This documentation never provides real-world chemical instructions; the regimen is fictional.
- Narrative effects: improved focus, stress tolerance, emotional suppression, operational endurance, heightened performance.
- Long-term neurological consequences: dependence, sleep disruption, memory instability, threat-perception distortion, aggression dysregulation, hallucination, psychotic collapse.
- The Hand was the earliest prime candidate, the strongest responder, the operational benchmark, the commander around whom the unit expanded.
- The unit believed its capability came from training and discipline. It did not know it was being experimentally altered.

## 17. Hidden Hand's True Operations

The unit believed it served legitimate national-security objectives. Later evidence reveals deliberate misrepresentation.

- Some operations were misrepresented: protecting criminal interests, eliminating witnesses, removing competitors for illicit organizations, enabling trafficking or covert criminal enterprises, recovering assets for hidden clients, performing deniable assassinations under false intelligence.
- NOT every action was meaningless: they genuinely saved people, genuinely protected teammates, sometimes produced good outcomes.
- The corruption sits at the level of: mission purpose, intelligence, hidden beneficiary, chain of command.
- NARRATIVELY MANDATORY: «The institution may have been false. The brotherhood was not.»
- All executive/government figures involved in the conspiracy are FICTION. Never identify real administrations or real officeholders as participants.

## 18. Collapse and Conviction

- Leaving the controlled logistics chain stops the covert dosage. The Hand does not know this. He suffers severe fictional neurological rebound/withdrawal and believes he is simply losing his mind.
- Symptoms: insomnia, paranoia, missing time, memory fragmentation, hallucinations, aggression, loss of emotional control.
- A catastrophic civilian incident follows. People die. His family may be among the dead. The exact sequence remains partially uncertain during much of the game.
- Do not automatically absolve him: he committed real acts. The deeper question is how much the secret program contributed, knew, manipulated, or exploited.
- He is arrested, tried, convicted, and sentenced toward death row. His last stable identity: «convicted murderer awaiting execution.»
- During imprisonment he retains one meaningful possession: THE HIDDEN HAND PHOTOGRAPH — a worn photograph of himself and the unit. It persists throughout the game as a narrative object, not disposable inventory.

## 19. Black Hand

His case is secretly flagged: authorities recognize the former prime subject, longest exposure, extraordinary performance, severe separation response, neurological anomaly, and legal disposability after conviction.

- Instead of normal execution/disposition he is secretly diverted to an isolated Alaska research complex.
- The later covert program: BLACK HAND — attempts to understand previous conditioning, exploit his altered nervous system, erase/reconstruct identity, recondition capabilities, and convert condemned/erased subjects into controllable deniable assets.

## 35. Close-Quarters Combat Architecture (Directive 008)

Frozen architecture for CQC before any implementation. Systems are not built under this section — it fixes what D009+ may implement.

### 35.1 Combat Identity
- CQC sits BETWEEN direct player control and trained-character execution assistance.
- THE PLAYER OWNS: intent (choosing to enter, press, or leave combat), movement, target selection, timing, defensive choice, aggression level, positioning, and the disengagement decision.
- THE CHARACTER'S TRAINING OWNS: the exact technical execution (which technique, its form and recovery) — chosen on the player's behalf from an internal library that stays consistent with the current reclamation band (§38).
- The execution library is DIRECTIONAL-READ-BASED, not memorized combos: deep but few attack archetypes; depth from reads, timing, spacing, and counters (influence: Absolver, doctrine §33).
- FORBIDDEN: combo-string fighter designs, QTE combat, long memorized move lists, arcade health-sponge exchanges.
- Combat emphasizes: distance, timing, initiative, balance/posture, stamina, injury, environment, positional advantage, control, escape, incapacitation.
- Real-world combatives appear ONLY at game-design abstraction level; never instructional real-world lethal-combat procedures.

### 35.2 Canonical Battle-State Model (BV-D033)
- CONTACT LAYER (authority state machine of the combat system):
  COMBAT_READ → ENGAGE → EXCHANGE → (DEFLECT | EVADE | COUNTER | BREAK | DISENGAGE) → ADVANTAGE → CONTROL → RESOLUTION → RESET
- ADVANTAGE = transient initiative ownership (who controls current tempo), NOT a global stun debuff.
- CONTROL = dominant positional state (clinch, pin, environmental control, restraint) — a real gameplay layer, not an afterimage.
- RESOLUTION = decisive outcome (incapacitation, escape, environmental/improvised finish, breakaway).
- RESET = return to COMBAT_READ or full disengage.
- SPATIAL LAYER (orthogonal): ranges STRIKING / CLOSE / CLINCH / ENVIRONMENTAL / GROUND / DISENGAGE. Transitions are seamless and positional, never cinematic locks; the player never loses locomotion agency to a script (doctrine §7 distinctness preserved).

### 35.3 Range and Flow
- Seamless movement between striking range, close range, clinch/control, environmental interaction, takedown/ground situations, and disengagement.
- Environmental interaction is a first-class range (wall slams, improvised objects, furniture) — not a scripted QTE.
- Break/disengage are first-class escape tools that trade posture/stamina for spacing and feed RESOLUTION (ESCAPE).

### 35.4 The Early-Game Hand and Rage (BV-D034)
- Identity: physically dangerous, prison-hardened, rage-prone, reckless under pressure, technically inconsistent, capable of frightening flashes of procedural memory — far less disciplined than his former self.
- He must feel DANGEROUS AND UNCONTROLLED, never incompetent.
- When calm, fragments of former operator technique remain.
- When trapped, hurt, frightened, humiliated, overwhelmed, or neurologically destabilized, survival aggression takes over.
- Early fight language (game abstractions only): headbutt-type attacks, rough clinch strikes, environmental slams, improvised-object attacks, desperate low-line attacks, uncontrolled follow-through, aggressive grappling, wall/furniture interaction.
- RAGE IS A MECHANIC, not a resource to spend: an emergent state (Control threshold + triggering event such as threat, loss, humiliation, or strain). It makes him more immediately violent but tactically worse. Cost set: overcommitment, increased stamina expenditure, poor threat reassessment, degraded defensive discipline, tunnel vision, reduced disengagement quality, increased neural destabilization, greater likelihood of excessive force.

### 35.5 Reclamation Orientation
- CQC progression is RECLAMATION, not acquisition of arbitrary new fighting abilities (doctrine §3): the player is recovering control over abilities that largely existed before the game.
- Framework bands (BV-D040): BROKEN/PRISONER → RELEARNING/SURVIVOR → OPERATOR → THE HAND (conceptual; mechanics in §38).

### 35.6 CQC System Scope
- TELEKINESIS-IN-CQC BOUNDARY (doctrine §12): TK augments combat, never replaces it. Uses allowed: creating openings, interrupting exchanges, retrieving or moving nearby objects, destabilizing environmental situations, emergency separation, momentary restraint/disruption. Every use incurs Neural Strain. FORBIDDEN: superhero-scale combat, infinite object spam, TK trivializing positional CQC.
- MINIMUM PHYSICAL SYSTEMS (doctrine §13): STAMINA, BALANCE/POSTURE, a small INJURY abstraction, pain/stagger, temporary impairment, environmental collision consequence. A few deep variables over many meters; survival doctrine §30 (decision-driven, not busywork) applies. No simulation bloat.

### 35.7 AI-Facing Combat Interfaces (doctrine §14)
- The combat architecture must EXPOSE — not hide — for later AI participation: range state, exchange window data (telegraph/active/recovery from one source), initiative ownership (ADVANTAGE), CONTROL reservation/break conditions, posture/balance, stamina, injury state, and the player's current defensive choice.
- All exposed via observable, legible events (SOP-006), never internal reads.
- Enemy types later (NOT implemented now): ordinary desperate humans, guards, trained Black Hand personnel, Shades, former Hidden Hand operators, specialized bosses, anomalous threats.
- Former Hidden Hand opponents must be able to recognize/exploit habits from the Hand's former combat doctrine (doctrine §27) — interfaces must expose enough for authored mirrors.

## 36. Control and Neural Strain (Directive 008)

### 36.1 Control (BV-D035)
- DEFINITION: The Hand's capability to govern anger, tunnel vision, aggression, procedural execution, decision-making, telekinetic restraint, and perception under stress.
- Control is NOT morality. Low Control is not weakness and not "evil".
- LOW-CONTROL SITUATIONAL BENEFITS: aggressive commitment, intimidation, resistance to hesitation, brutal improvisation.
- LOW-CONTROL TACTICAL COSTS: overcommitment, poor disengagement, degraded defense, heightened Strain sensitivity, excessive-force risk.
- PRESENTATION (recommended and frozen): internally a continuous value; player-facing as DISCRETE READABLE BANDS (e.g., COMPOSED / FRAGILE / BLEEDING / RAGE) delivered primarily through diegetic feedback (breathing, tremor, stance, camera, audio) with only a thin HUD anchor. Never a precise happy-gauge; never a mana bar.

### 36.2 Neural Strain (BV-D036)
- DEFINITION: independent of Control; produced by combinations of telekinesis, injury, psychological trauma, anomalous exposure, extreme stress, and memory/perception events.
- CONSEQUENCES: tinnitus, visual distortion, tremor, memory intrusion, auditory contamination, unreliable perception, motor disruption, involuntary telekinetic effects, reality/memory overlap.
- Neural Strain is NOT a renamed mana bar. Telekinesis is always present; the system governs cost, stability, and consequence — not whether the protagonist possesses the ability.
- Accumulates and decays with rest; the heavy path may open authored memory/perception events (BV-SKILL-018) and the temporary-vulnerability window (BV-SKILL-019 pipeline).

### 36.3 Control × Neural Strain Interaction (BV-D037)
- The model is a 2×2 of PLAY STATES — different play, not stacked penalties:
  - HIGH CONTROL + LOW STRAIN = deliberate operator state (precise, restrained execution; clean windows).
  - HIGH CONTROL + HIGH STRAIN = mentally disciplined but neurologically overloaded (execution held; perception/motor unreliable; intermittent distortion).
  - LOW CONTROL + LOW STRAIN = rage/aggression without major anomalous destabilization (violent and overcommitted, but perception intact).
  - LOW CONTROL + HIGH STRAIN = the highest-risk state: tunnel vision, memory contamination, telekinetic instability, poor judgment.
- INTERDEPENDENCY: higher Strain feeds Control decay (destabilization → more volatility); low Control makes the Hand more likely to escalate events that raise Strain (violence, TK misuse, bad decisions); Control recovery is easier under low Strain and harder under high Strain unless the Compound (§37) is active.
- RAGE TRIGGERS: Control at/below threshold AND a host event (threat/loss/humiliation/strain spike) — emergent, never a spendable resource.

### 36.4 Player Feedback (doctrine §15)
- Prefer restrained, diegetic techniques: breathing, heartbeat, hand tremor, stance changes, camera behavior, audio narrowing, peripheral distortion, animation behavior, thin HUD indicator.
- Avoid excessive screen effects; tablet comfort, accessibility, and readability are mandatory.

## 37. The Compound Branch (Directive 008)

### 37.1 The Compound
- A FICTIONAL, later gameplay branch. Non-procedural.
- Historically it helped REGULATE the neurological/emotional condition created/exploited by the program. It did not teach The Hand to fight; it did not originally create his training.

### 37.2 Compound-Dependent Progression
- POTENTIAL BENEFITS: faster Control recovery, reduced Control volatility, increased telekinetic precision, reduced Neural Strain accumulation, access to higher sustained telekinetic output, easier recovery of old Hidden Hand execution quality.
- POTENTIAL COSTS: dependency; progressively worse instability when unavailable; severe fictional withdrawal/crash; greater reliance on the system that originally controlled him; narrative consequences.
- NEVER reduced to a binary morality choice.

### 37.3 Independent Path
- Off-Compound progression is HARDER BUT FUNDAMENTALLY DIFFERENT: the Hand gradually learns genuine self-regulation.
- Integration of: Hidden Hand discipline + prison survival brutality + post-experiment telekinetic awareness.
- May carry a LOWER effortless/sustained telekinetic ceiling than the Compound-dependent version, but gains: greater independence, stable self-control, more adaptable CQC, less catastrophic dependency risk, eventual fusion of both combat identities.
- The late-game independent Hand does not recreate his old self — he becomes something the original Hidden Hand operator never was.

### 37.4 Discovery (diegetic)
- The Compound is first experienced diegetically, never through a perk-menu explanation.
- Preferred direction: the player encounters a recognizable dose during a severe destabilization event; using it produces an unmistakable transition (noise recedes, vision stabilizes, motor control improves, rage becomes manageable, telekinesis becomes clean, former operator precision resurfaces) BEFORE the cost is understood.

## 41. Visual & Equipment Doctrine (Directive 010)

Design-language freeze for BLACK VECTOR's human military forces — before production assets or enemy families
proliferate. This section fixes what asset production may later instantiate; it implements nothing itself.

### 41.1 Reference Acceptance and North Star (BV-D042)
The uploaded visual reference stack is accepted as design LANGUAGE ONLY — never a source of copyable assets.
Accepted reading order (North Star):
GROUNDED MILITARY FIRST → SELECTIVE CYBERNETIC ESCALATION → ROLE-SPECIFIC SILHOUETTES →
DIGITAL-CAMOUFLAGE / STEALTH LAYERS → MODULAR ARMOR → RESTRAINED ILLUMINATED TECHNOLOGY → VISIBLE HUMAN DAMAGE
→ SHADE RETRACTABLE HELMET + ASSAULT-RIFLE PRIORITY.
North-star test: any human-military design must read as recognizable tactical equipment that EVOLVED, with advanced
technology integrated into that equipment — never pasted on, never costume-clean. The game stays military-authentic
first, advanced technology second, anomalous/paranormal last.

### 41.2 Global Armor Visual Language (BV-D043)
All armor evolves from recognizable tactical equipment. Allowable substrate: cold-weather tactical clothing; digital
camouflage; soft-shell / insulated layers; plate carriers; load-bearing equipment; modular pouches; hard armor only
where functionally justified; helmets with optics/sensors; field repairs and wear; exposed cables/interfaces only
where required. Advanced technology must look INTEGRATED into military equipment. Restrained illuminated accents only
where they communicate real system state. FORBIDDEN: decorative neon, generic cyberpunk soldiers, superhero armor,
full mecha silhouettes, interchangeable "future operator" designs.

### 41.3 The Hand — Hidden Hand Military Silhouette (BV-D044-adjacent, north star from approved concept)
Old-era Hidden Hand version of The Hand (prologue, doctrine §29): founder/leader, sniper, overwatch specialist,
reconnaissance-assassin, protective battlefield orchestrator.
- Uniform language communicates MOBILITY + CONCEALMENT + PRECISION + asymmetric assassin-like military styling; NOT
  heavy assault armor. The approved military concept is the visual north star; this section does NOT redesign him.
- Permanent character details remain as established: long dark auburn/brown hair near the bottom of the ears; dark
  mystical green eyes; scar above the right eye; black/silver gauged earrings; the established tattoo system;
  lean/stocky athletic frame; damaged, human appearance.
- This section freezes ONLY how his old military equipment differs from later prison/scavenged equipment:
  - OLD-ERA (prologue): fitted component kit, precise layering, minimal drag, concealment-first color, overwatch kit
    (rifle, optics, observation/surveillance tools), maintained modularity.
  - LATER (present): stripped uniform remnants, prison/survivor substitution, improvised repair, scavenged gear layered
    over the old silhouette. The transition reads as HISTORY, not a costume swap.

### 41.4 Shade Baseline — Canonical Visual Foundation (BV-D044)
- Shade is VISIBLY HUMAN: exposed human face outside combat; visible implant/conditioning damage; scarring around
  neural-interface areas; exhausted/flattened human expression. Not a robot, not an android, not a faceless cyborg
  archetype.
- Retractable segmented combat helmet: deploys for battle; stores into collar / upper-back / temple architecture; the
  temple storage integrates with the skull-interface augmentation zone (one continuous command-and-control architecture).
- RIFLE IS THE PRIMARY WEAPON — the assault rifle defines him more than the blade. Compact sidearm optional. A compact
  powered/stun/vibro-style blade MAY remain as a secondary emergency CQC tool. FORBIDDEN: katana silhouette, samurai
  visual dominance.
- Armor communicates: a CONTAINED HUMAN BEING inside a command-and-control system.
- BOUNDARY: enemy Shade rifle doctrine does NOT alter the player's starting lock (doctrine §4/§9) — enemy equipment is
  not player gear; the rifle remains recovered capability for The Hand.

### 41.5 Shade Armor Tiers (BV-D045)
- LOW-TIER SHADE — mass-issued, neural-linked, practical, minimal individuality, shared tactical architecture.
- SPECIALIST SHADE — role-specific optics/sensors; reconnaissance, breaching, assault, electronic warfare, support.
- COMMAND SHADE — no implanted command dependency; externally controlled subordinate network; more individualized
  armor; wrist/hand command system; higher autonomy.
- COMPANION SHADE (the player's Shade) — visually derived from the lower-tier system; repaired, damaged, modified over
  time; progressively individualized equipment; retains unmistakable evidence he came from the Shade network.
- Tiers are NOT heavier toward the top; difference comes from PURPOSE, not weight.

### 41.6 Role-Based Armor Language (BV-D046)
Original role families inspired by the reference stack (not reproductions): STEALTH/RECON · ASSAULT · CONTAINMENT ·
SNIPER/OVERWATCH · TECHNICAL/ELECTRONIC · COMMANDER · HEAVY SUPPORT · COLD-WEATHER PATROL · FACILITY SECURITY ·
RECOVERY/EXTRACTION.
Each family is defined by a coherent armature: silhouette logic; armor distribution; headgear language; clothing layer;
sensor package; equipment density; mobility expectation; weather adaptation. Families are distinguishable at a glance
WITHOUT color coding; no palette-swapped clones.

### 41.7 Digital-Camouflage / Soft-Suit Language (BV-D043)
A lighter stealth architecture inspired by digital-camouflage reference language. Roles: recon, infiltrator, facility
scout, observation specialist, fast-response operator. Textile-first construction; flexible protective inserts;
localized hard armor; sensor integration; believable cold-weather layering. Visibly military — never superhero
spandex. ORIGINAL desaturated pixel-noise patterning only; no reproduction of any real-world protected camouflage
pattern.

### 41.8 Cybernetic Augmentation Limits (BV-D047)
Cyber-assassin reference material is a TECHNOLOGY CEILING, never the default. Allowed zones: neck; skull interface;
forearms; knees; lower legs; spine-support areas; sensor mounts. Human anatomy stays readable. Whole-body machinery
appears only for a narrative subject that justifies it; the default human is never obscured into a machine.

### 41.9 Faction Separation (BV-D048)
- HIDDEN HAND — elite, understated, individualized, assassin/recon influence, human-led excellence.
- BLACK HAND SECURITY — institutional, standardized, clinical/military, containment-oriented.
- SHADES — networked, unnervingly coordinated, technologically integrated; less individual at lower tiers.
- MERCENARY / CONTRACTOR — mixed commercial hardware, less standardized, pragmatic.
- BROKEN / ABANDONED SUBJECTS — degraded remnants of former systems, damaged equipment, scavenged replacements,
  visible history.
Distinguish by silhouette, layering, and material signature — never by bright faction colors. No production models
under this directive (BV-D052).

### 41.10 Alaska Integration (BV-D043)
Every armor family must account for: cold, wetness, salt exposure, snow, wind, darkness, maritime conditions, mud,
industrial interiors, remote maintenance constraints. Technology must look SERVICEABLE in that environment; perfect
showroom armor is prohibited except where narratively intentional (freeze authority: BV-D043).

### 41.11 Wear / Damage States (BV-D049)
Shared visual condition spectrum: PRISTINE → SERVICEABLE → DAMAGED → CRITICAL → FAILED. Armor visibly: scratches,
stains, tears, delamination, lost attachments, exposed underlayers, field repair. Shade equipment accumulates history
over time; the companion Shade becomes visually more individual as the player modifies and repairs his equipment.

### 41.12 Technology Escalation Ladder (BV-D050)
- TIER 0 — CONVENTIONAL: recognizable military equipment.
- TIER 1 — ENHANCED: integrated optics, sensors, comms, biometric systems.
- TIER 2 — NETWORKED: neural links, synchronized tactical data, command interfaces.
- TIER 3 — EXPERIMENTAL: advanced adaptive armor, nanobite-linked systems, unusual field technology.
- TIER 4 — ANOMALOUS INTERFACE: extremely rare technology interacting with phenomena Black Hand does not fully
  understand. TIER 4 MUST NEVER BECOME EVERYDAY EQUIPMENT.

### 41.13 Color / Material Discipline (BV-D051)
Prefer: charcoal, black, graphite, dark olive, worn gray, cold-weather muted tones, desaturated digital camo,
oxidized metal, wet technical fabric. Accent lighting is sparse and functional only. Factions may carry subtle
material/signature distinctions but may never be identifiable only by bright colors.

### 41.14 Originality Boundaries (BV-D042)
The uploaded images are reference language only. Do NOT reproduce any specific armor, helmet, character, or commercial
design. Extract: layering, silhouette, equipment density, cybernetic integration, material balance, camouflage
treatment, helmet concepts — then design ORIGINAL BLACK VECTOR variants.

### 41.15 Companion Shade Visual Evolution (BV-D049)
Over time the companion Shade accrues: field repair, individualized pouches, swapped plates, sensor refinements,
weathering consistent with the wear/damage spectrum (§41.11) — while retaining the underpinning low-tier Shade
architecture so his origin in the network stays readable. Evolution is diegetic (capability + repair history), never
cosmetic chrome.

### 41.16 Ownership and Boundaries
- Ownership: `BV-SKILL-022 visual-equipment-doctrine` is the selectable domain skill for any character/armor/equipment
  visual decision.
- Directive 010 fixes DESIGN LANGUAGE ONLY. It authorizes nothing in-game: no models, textures, shaders, armor
  inventory, damage visuals, equipment stats, weapons, animations, Shade AI, nanobites, helmet animation, UI, or
  combat logic (BV-D052).
- Resolved boundaries: enemy rifle presence vs player starting lock (§41.4); real-world camouflage patterns (original
  patterning only, §41.7); temple-helmet storage vs skull interface (§41.4).
